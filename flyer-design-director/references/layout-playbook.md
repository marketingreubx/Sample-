# Layout Playbook

How Pinterest-level flyer designers place subjects, angles and text. Pick an archetype first, then an angle, then place type around the subject's silhouette.

## 1. Layout archetypes

| # | Archetype | Looks like | Text placement | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Type Behind Subject** | Huge headline across the middle; cut-out subject stands in front, hiding part of the letters | Headline mid-to-upper band, 70–100% width, behind head/shoulders; sub + CTA bottom band in front | Personal brands, service providers, "the face of the brand" |
| 2 | **Frame Break / Pop-out** | Photo inside a rectangle/arch/phone; head, hand or product pokes out over the frame edge | Headline above frame; offer badge overlapping frame corner; CTA below | Promos, before/after, app/social media offers |
| 3 | **Split Diagonal** | Canvas cut on a 10–25° diagonal; photo one side, solid colour the other | Text on the solid side, aligned to the diagonal or flush left | Sales, events, energetic offers |
| 4 | **Low-Angle Hero** | Shot from below, subject towers, sky/ceiling behind | Headline top (sky = clean space), CTA bottom corner | Authority, coaches, "boss" energy |
| 5 | **Top-Down Flat Lay** | Products/props arranged from directly above | Text in a clear central card or a deliberate gap in the arrangement | Product launches, kits, skincare routines |
| 6 | **Macro Detail** | Extreme close-up (skin, drop of serum, lashes) fills canvas | Small, elegant type in a corner; big single word optional | Luxury, results, "glow" messaging |
| 7 | **Editorial Magazine Cover** | Subject centred, masthead top, cover lines on sides | Masthead top 15%; 3–5 cover lines left/right thirds; price/date bottom | Premium, education, launches |
| 8 | **Collage / Scrapbook** | Torn paper, tape, polaroids, stickers, handwritten notes | Text on paper strips and stickers, slightly rotated (±3–8°) | Gen-Z, UGC, behind-the-scenes, lists |
| 9 | **Before / After Split** | Vertical or horizontal 50/50 with divider line | "BEFORE"/"AFTER" labels in corners; result headline across divider | Facials, acne, brows, lashes, social growth |
| 10 | **Floating 3D / Levitation** | Products and elements floating at different depths with shadows | Headline centre or top, products orbit it | Product drops, bundles, offers |
| 11 | **Big Number / Price Hero** | Oversized number or price dominates; subject small or cut-out beside it | The number IS the layout; supporting text tucked into its counters/sides | Discounts, "3 spots left", stats |
| 12 | **Phone Mockup** | Hand holding phone or floating phone showing a feed/results | Headline top; results/metrics as floating cards around phone | Social media management, DM offers, apps |

## 2. Camera angles and what they say

| Angle | Emotion | Lens / framing cue for prompts |
|---|---|---|
| Eye level | Trust, friendly | 85mm, chest-up, eyes on upper third |
| Low angle (worm's-eye) | Power, authority, aspiration | 24–35mm, camera at waist/knee height looking up |
| High angle | Soft, approachable, product-overview | 35–50mm, camera above looking down 30–45° |
| Top-down (90°) | Organised, aesthetic, flat lay | 50mm overhead, flat even light |
| Dutch tilt (10–20°) | Energy, urgency, fashion | Any lens, "tilted dutch angle" |
| 3/4 profile | Elegant, editorial, skin-focused | 85–105mm, face turned 45°, jawline lit |
| Over-the-shoulder | Story, service-in-action | 35mm, practitioner's shoulder in foreground |
| Macro | Detail, luxury, results | 100mm macro, shallow DOF f/2.8 |
| Wide environmental | Place, lifestyle, studio tour | 24mm, subject small in frame, room visible |
| Fisheye / close wide | Playful, Gen-Z, bold | 14–16mm, face close to lens, distorted |

Rule: a flyer's angle should match its promise. Authority = low angle. Gentle care = high angle or 3/4 soft light. Results = macro or before/after at identical angles.

## 3. Text placement rules

- **Grid**: think 3×3. Subject on left or right third; headline in the opposite two-thirds or behind the subject. Eyes ideally on the upper-third line.
- **Look direction**: the subject should look or point *toward* the text or CTA, never off the canvas.
- **Hierarchy (max 4 levels)**: Headline (largest) → Sub-headline (40–50% of headline) → Body/bullets (25–30%) → CTA/handle (bold but small, high-contrast pill).
- **Sizes on 1080 wide**: headline 120–260px, sub 48–72px, body 30–40px, CTA 34–44px, legal/handle 24–28px.
- **Z-pattern** for text-heavy flyers (top-left → top-right → bottom-left → bottom-right CTA). **F-pattern** for lists. **Centre stack** for minimal luxury.
- **Behind-the-subject effect**: put headline on a layer below the cut-out subject; let the head cover ≤ 30% of the letters so the word is still readable (hide the middle of a long word, never the first letter).
- **Edge tension**: let 1–2 elements bleed off the edge (huge letter, product, hand) to imply the image continues.
- **Badges**: circle/starburst with price or "NEW", rotated 8–15°, overlapping the subject's edge or frame corner.
- **CTA**: always bottom third, pill or underline, with an arrow/hand icon pointing to it if space allows.

## 4. Depth and polish tricks (what makes it look "designer")

1. Subject cut-out with a soft drop shadow (opacity 25–35%, blur 40–60px, offset down).
2. Outline/stroke text duplicated behind solid text (offset 6–12px) for a 3D echo.
3. Repeating headline as a faded background pattern (opacity 5–10%).
4. Glassmorphism card: white 15–25% opacity + background blur for text over photos.
5. Film grain/noise 3–6% over the whole design to unify AI and real photos.
6. Colour-matched gradient map so subject and background share a tone.
7. Hand-drawn arrows, circles, underlines, sparkles near the key word.
8. Tape/sticker labels for lists and prices.
9. A secondary small photo in a circle or arch (practitioner headshot, product).
10. Light leak or soft glow behind the subject's head to separate from background.

## 5. Typography pairings (free on Google Fonts / Canva)

| Mood | Headline | Body |
|---|---|---|
| Luxury / spa | Playfair Display Italic, Cormorant Garamond, Bodoni Moda | Montserrat Light, Jost |
| Bold / sales | Anton, Bebas Neue, Archivo Black, Druk-style (Oswald Bold) | Inter, Poppins |
| Modern clean | Syne, Clash Display (Fontshare), Space Grotesk | DM Sans |
| Soft feminine | Bricolage Grotesque, Fraunces, Gloock | Nunito, Quicksand |
| Editorial | DM Serif Display, Abril Fatface | Work Sans |
| Handwritten accent | Caveat, Reenie Beanie, Nanum Pen | — |

Mix rule: one display font + one sans + optional script accent (one or two words only). Tight tracking (-2 to -4%) on big display headlines; wide tracking (+10–20%) on small caps labels.

## 6. Colour systems

- **60/30/10**: 60% background, 30% subject/secondary, 10% accent (CTA, badges, highlight word).
- Pull the accent from the photo (lipstick, product cap, wardrobe) so it feels designed, not random.
- Dark backgrounds make skin and products glow; light/neutral backgrounds feel clinical-clean.
- Highlight one word in the headline in the accent colour or italic serif.

## 7. Format specs

| Placement | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| IG/FB feed | 1080×1350 (4:5) | Best real estate in feed |
| IG square | 1080×1080 | Keep key text in centre 900px |
| Story / Reel cover | 1080×1920 | Safe area: 250px top, 340px bottom |
| Pinterest | 1000×1500 (2:3) | Text-heavy works well |
| Print flyer | A5 / US Letter, 300dpi, 3mm bleed | Upscale AI images ×2–4 |
| Facebook/Meta ad | 1080×1350 + 1080×1920 | Keep text < ~20% for reach |

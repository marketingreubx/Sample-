# Niche Pack: Estheticians, Med Spas, Salons & Social Media Agencies

## A. Esthetician / skincare / med spa

**Default palettes**
| Vibe | Hex |
|---|---|
| Clean luxe nude | #F4EDE4 bg, #D9C2AE secondary, #8A6E5A primary, #1E1A17 text, #C9A46A gold accent |
| Blush glow | #FBEDEE, #F2C6C9, #C0727B, #2B1E21, #E8B7A0 |
| Spa sage | #EEF0E8, #B9C4A8, #6F7E5E, #1F2419, #D8C3A5 |
| Dark luxury | #121212, #2A2522, #C9A46A gold, #F5EFE8 text, #8C5A4A |
| Clinical clean | #FFFFFF, #E9F1F4, #4C7A8A, #1A2A30, #F2B8A0 |

**Concept starters**
1. *Glow Before/After* — identical-angle macro cheek shots, divider line, "4 SESSIONS. ZERO FILTER."
2. *Type Behind Client* — glowing-skin client 3/4 profile, giant serif "GLOW" behind head, offer badge "$99 FIRST FACIAL".
3. *Hands-in-Action* — over-shoulder service shot, headline in negative space, "Book your reset".
4. *Floating Ritual* — serum, cleanser, jade roller, rose petals floating; "Your 3-step glow ritual".
5. *Macro Skin* — dewy skin macro with water droplets, tiny elegant type "Real skin. Real results."
6. *Holiday/Event Promo* — dutch-angle playful shot, sticker badges, deadline "ENDS SUNDAY".

**Prompt vocabulary**: dewy luminous skin, glass skin, hydrated glow, soft clamshell lighting, spa treatment room with white towels, LED light therapy mask glowing red, hydrafacial wand, gua sha, jade roller, frosted glass bottles, travertine pedestals, eucalyptus sprigs, warm beige linen.

**Compliance**: no medical claims you can't prove; before/after must be real client photos with consent (use AI only for mood/branding, not fake results); add "results may vary".

## B. Social media management agency / coach (socialreubx, estheticsbizgenius)

**Default palettes**
| Vibe | Hex |
|---|---|
| Bold creator | #0F0F10 bg, #FF4F8B accent, #FFD84D highlight, #FFFFFF text |
| Clean premium | #F6F4F1, #111111, #3E5BFF accent, #FFB800 highlight |
| Beauty-agency hybrid | #F7EFEA, #1C1C1C, #D4A373, #E07A8A |

**Concept starters**
1. *Phone Mockup Results* — hand holding iPhone showing an esthetician's Instagram grid; floating cards "+312 followers", "28 bookings from DMs".
2. *Big Number* — "$3K/mo" or "30 DAYS" oversized; founder cut-out beside it; "Done-for-you content for estheticians".
3. *Low-Angle Authority* — founder low angle, arms crossed, city/studio behind; "I fill esthetician calendars."
4. *Collage Behind-the-Scenes* — polaroids of shoots, content calendar, sticky notes; "What $3K/month of content looks like".
5. *Problem/Solution Split* — left: empty booking calendar (desaturated); right: full calendar (vivid); "Posting ≠ booking. Strategy does."

**Hooks for headlines**: "Your feed is costing you clients", "Stop posting. Start booking.", "Booked out in 60 days", "Estheticians: steal this content plan", "3 spots open for [month]".

**Prompt vocabulary**: modern creative studio, ring light and camera on tripod, MacBook with analytics dashboard, iPhone showing Instagram profile, content calendar on wall, confident entrepreneur, smart casual blazer, warm daylight loft.

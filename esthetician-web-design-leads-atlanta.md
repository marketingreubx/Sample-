# Web Design Leads: Atlanta Estheticians (Batch 1 of 5)

Researched October 4, 2026. Sources: Google Maps (filtered for 4.5★+ with no website listed), Instagram profiles, VoyageATL interviews, Yelp/Birdeye/Fresha listings.

**How these were picked:** I started with 22 Atlanta skincare businesses that Google says have no website. Then I checked each one by hand. 5 of them actually *did* have a site (Define Skin Studio, SKNZ Studios, Esthetic Principles, HerFace, Judith of Budapest), so they were dropped. The 5 below are the ones that held up: real clients, real reviews, active Instagram, and **no real branded website**.

> ⚠️ Before you send anything, open each IG profile on your phone and confirm the details still match. Profiles change. This matters most for Lead #4 (the broken link).

# Outreach scripts

**Rules for these:**
1. The DM opens a conversation. **Don't pitch in message one.**
2. Lead with something you actually noticed about them. That's what keeps it from feeling like spam.
3. Offer a **free homepage mockup**. For web design, showing beats telling, and it's your best way to start a $3k conversation.
4. Swap `[Your Name]` and `[link]` before sending.

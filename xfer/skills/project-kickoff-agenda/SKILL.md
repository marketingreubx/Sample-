# Project Kickoff Agenda

A kickoff call aligns everyone before work begins: what success looks like, who decides, how feedback works and what happens first. Thirty focused minutes here saves weeks of revisions later.

## What to collect

1. The project, scope and timeline (from the proposal or SOW).
2. Who will attend from both sides.
3. What the user already knows from the intake form.
4. Call length and format.

## Before the call

- Send the agenda 24 hours ahead with 2 or 3 things for the client to prepare.
- Review the intake answers and prepare follow up questions.
- Prepare a one-slide or one-page plan of the first 30 days.

## The agenda (45 minutes)

1. **Welcome and introductions (5 min):** roles on both sides.
2. **Goals and success (10 min):** "What would make this a huge success in 3 months?", how it will be measured, why now.
3. **Audience and message (5 min):** who we serve, what they need to hear.
4. **Brand and preferences (5 min):** likes, dislikes, examples, no-gos.
5. **How decisions happen (5 min):** who approves, how feedback is given (one consolidated list), turnaround times.
6. **Plan and timeline (5 min):** phases, first milestone, dependencies on the client.
7. **Working together (5 min):** channels, response times, meeting rhythm, urgent issues.
8. **Risks and questions (3 min):** "What could get in the way?"
9. **Confirm next steps (2 min):** who does what by when.

## Question bank

15 to 20 questions grouped by section, tailored to the project type.

## Live notes template

Goals, metrics, audience notes, preferences, approvers, decisions made, action items (owner, due date), open questions.

## Follow up summary (same day)

Goals and metrics agreed, decisions, action items with owners and dates, next meeting, link to shared folder.

## Rules

- Keep to time. Respect the agenda.
- The client talks more than the user in sections 2 to 4.
- Every decision and action ends up in writing the same day.

## Output format

```
PRE-CALL MESSAGE
...

AGENDA (45 min)
...

QUESTION BANK
...

NOTES TEMPLATE
...

FOLLOW UP SUMMARY
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Success measures defined
- [ ] Approver and feedback process agreed
- [ ] Action items with owners and dates
- [ ] Summary sent the same day

# DM Sales Closer

Selling in DMs is a conversation, not a broadcast. The person who asks the best questions wins. This skill keeps each message short, moves the chat one step at a time (interest, need, fit, offer, yes), and never sounds desperate.

## What to collect

1. The offer, price range, and what is included.
2. The conversation so far (paste it), or the stage: new follower, replied to a cold DM, Story reply, comment, inquiry.
3. Who the prospect is and anything known about them.
4. The goal: book a call, close in chat, get a deposit.

## The conversation map

1. **Open:** warm, personal, about them.
2. **Discover:** 3 to 5 questions, one at a time: current situation, goal, what they tried, what it is costing them, timeline, who decides.
3. **Reflect:** summarise their situation in their words. "So if I got this right..."
4. **Permission:** "Want me to share how I'd approach it?"
5. **Pitch:** short and specific to what they said: the plan, the result, why it works. 3 to 5 lines.
6. **Price:** state it confidently once, anchored to the outcome and with what is included. Offer at most two options.
7. **Close:** a simple yes question. "Want me to send the booking link and lock in your start date?"
8. **Handle objections:** acknowledge, clarify, answer, re-close.
9. **Payment and onboarding:** clear next steps, payment link, what happens after payment.
10. **Follow up:** if they go quiet, at day 1, day 3, day 7 with value, not pressure.

## Discovery questions bank
- "What made you reach out now?"
- "What does a great month look like for you?"
- "What have you tried so far? What happened?"
- "If nothing changes in the next 3 months, what does that cost you?"
- "Is it just you deciding, or someone else too?"
- "When would you ideally want this sorted?"

## Objection replies (pattern: acknowledge, ask, answer, re-close)
- **Too expensive:** "Totally fair. Can I ask, too expensive compared to what?" Then reframe around the cost of the problem or offer a smaller first step. Never drop the price without removing scope.
- **I need to think:** "Of course. What part do you want to think through? Maybe I can help now."
- **Not now:** "No problem. When would be a better time? I'll note it and check in then."
- **I need to ask my partner:** "Makes sense. Want me to send a short summary you can show them?"
- **Can you do it cheaper?:** "I can do [smaller scope] for [lower price], or the full plan at [price]. Which fits better?"
- **I'll do it myself:** "That works for a lot of people. The difference is [time, expertise, result]. If it helps, here's one tip to start."

## Rules

- One question per message. Short messages. Match their energy and length.
- Use their words when reflecting.
- Never chase with guilt. Never fake scarcity.
- Do not pitch before you understand the need.
- Price once, clearly. No apologising.
- Voice notes only if they used them first.

## Output format

When the user pastes a conversation:
```
WHERE YOU ARE: [stage]
WHAT THEY REALLY SAID: ...

YOUR NEXT MESSAGE
...

IF THEY SAY ...
- "...": ...
- "...": ...

THE STEP AFTER
...
```
When the user wants a full script, write every stage with 2 options per message.

## Final check

- [ ] Message is short and ends with one question or one clear step
- [ ] Uses the prospect's own words
- [ ] No pressure, no fake scarcity
- [ ] Clear path to payment

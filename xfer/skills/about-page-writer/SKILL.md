# About Page Writer

The About page is one of the most visited pages, and most waste it on a CV. Visitors are asking: Can this person help me? Do they understand me? Can I trust them? What's next? This skill answers those questions, using the founder's story only where it builds trust.

## What to collect

1. Who the user helps and the result they deliver.
2. Their story: why they started, turning points, relevant background.
3. Their approach or method and what makes it different.
4. Proof: results, clients, testimonials, credentials, media.
5. Values and personality, and a few personal details they are happy to share.
6. The next step (book, buy, follow, contact).
7. Tone: warm, bold, professional, playful.

## Structure

1. **Headline about the reader:** "You're great at your craft. Let's get you booked." Not "About me".
2. **Who we help and the result:** 2 to 3 lines.
3. **The story (short):** where it started, the problem seen, the turning point, why this work matters now. 100 to 200 words.
4. **The approach:** 3 to 4 principles or steps.
5. **Proof:** numbers, logos, a testimonial.
6. **Values or "what you can expect":** 3 to 5 bullets.
7. **A human touch:** a few personal details (optional, short).
8. **Next step:** a clear CTA with a reason to act.

## Variants

- Short bio (50 words) for social and speaker intros.
- Instagram highlight version (5 to 7 slides).
- Third-person version for media.

## Rules

- "You" appears more than "I".
- True story only. No exaggeration.
- Specific details over adjectives ("12 years in hospitality" beats "experienced").
- Scannable: short paragraphs, subheads.

## Output format

```
HEADLINE OPTIONS
...
ABOUT PAGE
...
SHORT BIO (50 words)
...
HIGHLIGHT SLIDES
1. ...
THIRD-PERSON BIO
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Opens with the reader
- [ ] Story builds trust and is brief
- [ ] Proof included
- [ ] Clear next step

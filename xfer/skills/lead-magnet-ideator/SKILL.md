# Lead Magnet Ideator

A good lead magnet solves one narrow, urgent problem completely and quickly, and in doing so reveals the next problem that the user's paid offer solves. A bad one is a vague ebook that attracts people who never buy. This skill designs the first kind.

## What to collect

1. What the user sells, the price, and who buys it.
2. The buyer's biggest frustration in their own words, if known.
3. Where the magnet will be promoted (Instagram keyword DMs, ads, website, LinkedIn, WhatsApp status).
4. How it will be delivered (email tool, DM automation, Google Drive, website).
5. Time and skills the user has to create it this week.

## The method

### 1. Map the buyer's path
Write the 5 steps a buyer takes from "I have a problem" to "I need to hire someone". The magnet sits at the step just before the paid offer becomes the obvious next move.

### 2. Generate 10 ideas across formats
Use at least 6 formats: checklist, template or swipe file, calculator, quiz or scorecard, free audit or teardown, mini training (video under 15 minutes), challenge (3 to 5 days), case study breakdown, script pack, tool or spreadsheet, resource list.

### 3. Score each idea from 1 to 5
| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Specific | Does it solve one named problem? |
| Fast | Can they get a win in under 15 minutes? |
| Buyer filter | Would only real buyers want it? |
| Bridge | Does using it reveal the need for the paid offer? |
| Easy to make | Can the user build it in under 2 days? |
| Easy to promote | Can it be explained in 7 words? |

Pick the highest total. Break ties on Bridge.

### 4. Build the winner
- **Title:** outcome plus specificity plus speed. "The 9 Post Booking Calendar for Estheticians" beats "Social Media Tips".
- **Contents outline:** every section, with what the reader does in each.
- **The quick win:** the one thing they will finish in the first 10 minutes.
- **The bridge:** where and how the magnet points to the paid offer, naturally.
- **Landing or DM copy:** headline, 3 bullets, button text, and the DM keyword if Instagram.
- **Delivery message:** what they receive instantly.
- **Follow up:** 3 to 5 messages over 7 days: check they used it, share a result story, invite to a call or offer.

### 5. Promotion plan
Three posts or Reels that sell the magnet, one Story sequence, and the comment keyword trigger if used.

## Rules

- Narrow beats broad. One problem solved fully, not ten solved partly.
- The magnet must be genuinely useful even if they never buy.
- No fake scarcity or inflated "worth $497" claims.
- Never promise results the user cannot back up.
- Name it in the buyer's words, not marketer words.

# Client Offboarding and Handoff

The way a project ends is what the client remembers and repeats to others. A clean handoff, a clear summary of results and a thoughtful goodbye turn a finished project into testimonials, referrals and repeat work, even when the client is leaving unhappy.

## What to collect

1. The project, what was delivered, and results.
2. Why it is ending (project complete, client cancelling, budget, user ending it).
3. Assets and accounts involved.
4. Outstanding payments.
5. The relationship: happy, neutral, strained.

## The method

### 1. Handoff checklist
- Final files in an organised folder with clear names and editable sources if agreed.
- Logins: transfer ownership, remove the user's access, recommend the client change passwords.
- Documentation: how things work, schedules, recurring tasks, tools and costs.
- Training: a short video walkthrough or call if useful.
- Subscriptions and tools paid by the user: cancel or transfer.
- Calendar: ending date, last deliverables.

### 2. Results summary
Starting point, what was done, results with numbers, recommendations for the next 3 months.

### 3. Wrap up email
Thank them for something specific, link to the handoff folder, the results summary, final invoice details if any, next steps for them, and an open door.

### 4. The asks (timed)
- **Testimonial:** in the wrap up or 3 days later if the relationship is positive.
- **Referral:** after the testimonial.
- **Check-in:** 60 to 90 days later with a useful idea.

### 5. If the client is unhappy
Acknowledge, deliver everything professionally, skip the testimonial ask, ask for honest feedback instead, and learn from it.

### 6. Internal lessons
3 things to keep, 3 to change, update templates.

## Rules

- Hand everything over on time even if the ending is difficult.
- Never hold files hostage unless the agreement ties delivery to payment, and say so calmly.
- Security first with access removal.

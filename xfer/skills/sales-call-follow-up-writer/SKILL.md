# Sales Call Follow-Up Writer

Most deals are lost after the call, not on it. The recap should land the same day, prove you listened, and make the next step obvious. Follow ups should add value each time and end with a clean yes or no.

## What to collect

1. Call notes: their situation, goals, pains, budget signals, timeline, decision makers, objections, words they used.
2. What was offered (packages, price, guarantee) and what was agreed as the next step.
3. Channel for follow up (email, WhatsApp, DM).

## The recap email (same day)

1. Thank them for something specific they shared.
2. **Where you are:** 2 to 3 lines in their words.
3. **Where you want to be:** goals and why they matter.
4. **The plan:** recommended option in 3 steps.
5. **Investment:** the price and what is included, or link to the proposal.
6. **Next step:** one clear action with a date ("Reply YES and I'll send the invoice. We can start Monday.").
7. Optional P.S.: a relevant result or resource.

## The follow up sequence (if no decision)

- **Day 2:** short check: "Any questions after reading the plan?" plus one useful tip related to their goal.
- **Day 5:** proof: a short story of a similar client's result.
- **Day 8:** address the likely objection directly (price, timing, trust).
- **Day 12:** the clean close: "Should I keep your spot for [date] or close your file for now? Either is totally fine."
- **Day 30:** fresh start with a new idea or update.

## Special situations

- **"Still deciding":** ask what part is unclear, offer a 10 minute call, agree a decision date.
- **"Need to talk to my partner":** send a one page summary they can forward.
- **New objection by message:** acknowledge, clarify, answer, re-close.
- **They said no:** thank them, ask what made the decision (optional), leave the door open, ask for a referral if appropriate.

## Rules

- Same day recap, always.
- Every follow up adds something new. Never "just checking in" alone.
- Use their words from the call.
- No guilt, no fake urgency.

## Output format

```
RECAP EMAIL (send today)
Subject: ...
...

FOLLOW UPS
Day 2: ...
Day 5: ...
Day 8: ...
Day 12: ...
Day 30: ...

IF THEY SAY ...
- "Still deciding": ...
- "Need to ask my partner": ...
- "No thanks": ...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Recap uses their words
- [ ] One clear next step with a date
- [ ] Every follow up adds value
- [ ] Clean close at the end

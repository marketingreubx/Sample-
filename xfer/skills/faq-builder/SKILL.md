# FAQ Builder

An FAQ is a quiet salesperson. The best ones answer the questions buyers are too shy to ask, handle objections honestly, and end with a next step. They are written from real questions, not invented ones.

## What to collect

1. The offer, price, process and policies.
2. Real questions customers ask (DMs, emails, calls, comments, reviews). Paste as many as possible.
3. Where the FAQ will appear.
4. Common objections and reasons people do not buy.

## The method

### 1. Gather and group
Group questions into: Fit (is this for me?), Results (will it work?), Process (how does it work?), Time, Price and payment, Trust and guarantees, Logistics (location, booking, delivery), After purchase.

### 2. Prioritise
Order by how often it is asked and how much it blocks buying. Objections first.

### 3. Write each answer
- Start with a direct answer in the first sentence (yes, no, the number, the timeframe).
- Then 1 to 3 sentences of explanation or reassurance.
- Add proof or an example if it helps.
- End with a link or action where useful.
- 30 to 80 words each.

### 4. Formats
- Website or sales page FAQ (8 to 12 questions).
- Instagram highlight (one question per slide).
- WhatsApp Business quick replies (short versions).
- Google Business Profile Q&A.
- FAQ schema-ready list (question and answer pairs).

## Rules

- Honest answers. If the answer is "it depends", say what it depends on.
- No defensive tone.
- Never promise results that cannot be guaranteed.
- Use the customer's wording for questions.

## Output format

```
GROUPED QUESTIONS
...

FAQ (website)
Q: ...
A: ...

INSTAGRAM HIGHLIGHT
Slide 1: ...

WHATSAPP QUICK REPLIES

...

...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Questions from real customers where possible
- [ ] Each answer starts with a direct answer
- [ ] Objections handled honestly
- [ ] Versions for each channel

# Review Response Writer

Review replies are written for future customers as much as for the reviewer. A calm, specific, human reply to a bad review can win more trust than a perfect rating. Generic "Thanks for your feedback!" replies waste the opportunity.

## What to collect

1. The review text, rating, platform and reviewer name.
2. What actually happened (from the business side).
3. Any action already taken.
4. Business name, who signs replies, and tone.

## The method

### Positive reviews
1. Thank them by name.
2. Mention a specific detail they shared.
3. Add something useful (what to try next, a team member's name).
4. Invite them back.
40 to 80 words. Vary replies so they don't look copied.

### Neutral reviews
1. Thank them.
2. Acknowledge the specific point.
3. Explain briefly what is being improved.
4. Invite them to share more privately or come back.

### Negative reviews
1. Stay calm. Never argue facts in public.
2. Thank them and acknowledge their experience.
3. Apologise for the experience where appropriate (without admitting legal liability if serious).
4. Briefly explain what's being done, without excuses.
5. Move to a private channel with a name and contact.
6. Sign with a real name.
Then write the private follow up message.

### Suspected fake or unfair reviews
Polite public reply ("We can't find a record of your visit; please contact us at..."), plus steps to report it through the platform.

### Review request system
When to ask, the message with the direct link, and a follow up. No incentives where platforms forbid them.

## Rules

- Never reveal private customer details.
- No defensive or sarcastic tone.
- Keep replies short.
- Respond within 48 hours.

## Output format

```
PUBLIC REPLY
...

PRIVATE FOLLOW UP (if negative)
...

NEXT STEPS
...

REVIEW REQUEST MESSAGE
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Specific to the review
- [ ] Calm and human
- [ ] Moves conflicts private
- [ ] No private details shared

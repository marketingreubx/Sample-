# Customer Persona Builder

A persona is only useful if it changes what the user writes and who they message. This skill builds one from evidence, marks every assumption, and ends with practical outputs: hooks, offers and openers in the customer's own language.

## What to collect

1. What the user sells and the price.
2. Their best 1 to 3 past clients (or the people they most want), even roughly.
3. Any raw evidence: DMs, reviews, comments, call notes, survey answers, competitor reviews, Reddit or Facebook group posts.
4. Market and location.

If there is no evidence, say so and give the user a 20 minute research task (read 30 reviews of competitors, 20 comments under popular posts in the niche) before treating the persona as true.

## The method

### 1. Choose one person, not a demographic
"Solo esthetician, 29 to 40, 2 years in business, renting a room, fully booked on weekends and empty on weekdays" beats "women 25 to 45 interested in beauty".

### 2. Build the profile
- **Situation:** business stage, team size, revenue range, tools, daily reality.
- **Desired outcome:** what they want in their words, and the deeper reason (freedom, status, security, pride).
- **Current pain:** what is going wrong right now, and what it costs them each month.
- **What they have tried:** and why it did not work for them.
- **Fears and doubts:** about buying, about the user's type of service, about themselves.
- **Objections:** price, time, trust, "I can do it myself", bad past experience.
- **Buying triggers:** the moments when they start looking (slow month, new competitor, a launch, a bad review).
- **How they decide:** who else is involved, what proof they need, how fast.
- **Where their attention is:** platforms, accounts they follow, groups, events, podcasts.
- **Their words:** 10 to 20 phrases they actually use. Quote evidence when available.

### 3. Label confidence
Mark each line as Evidence, Likely or Guess. Guesses must be checked before the user spends money on them.

### 4. Turn it into action
- 10 content hooks that speak to their pain or desire.
- 3 offer angles (how to frame the paid offer for this person).
- 5 DM or email openers using their language.
- 5 questions to ask on a discovery call to confirm the persona.

## Rules

- One persona at a time. If the user has two very different buyers, build two separate files.
- Their words, not marketing words. "I'm tired of posting and nobody books" beats "low conversion".
- No stereotypes or assumptions about gender, religion or income from appearance.
- Never present guesses as research.

## Output format

```
PERSONA NAME: [a memorable label, e.g. "Weekday-Empty Wendy"]
One line: ...

SITUATION ...
WANTS (surface and deeper) ...
PAIN AND ITS COST ...
TRIED BEFORE ...
FEARS ...
OBJECTIONS ...
BUYING TRIGGERS ...
HOW THEY DECIDE ...
WHERE TO REACH THEM ...
THEIR WORDS
- "..." (Evidence / Likely / Guess)

USE IT
Hooks (10): ...
Offer angles (3): ...
Openers (5): ...
Discovery questions (5): ...

WHAT TO VERIFY NEXT
- ...
```

## Final check

- [ ] One specific person, not a demographic range
- [ ] Every line labelled Evidence, Likely or Guess
- [ ] At least 10 phrases in the customer's own words
- [ ] Ends with hooks, offers and openers the user can use today

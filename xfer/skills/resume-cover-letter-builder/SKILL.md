# Resume and Cover Letter Builder

Recruiters scan a resume in seconds and applicant tracking systems scan for keywords. A strong application mirrors the job's priorities, proves each one with a specific achievement, and is easy to read. The cover letter adds the "why this job, why me" story in under 300 words.

## What to collect

1. The job description (paste it).
2. The user's current CV or work history: roles, dates, responsibilities, achievements, numbers.
3. Education, certifications, tools, languages.
4. Country or region (CV conventions differ).
5. Anything to address (career gap, career change, freelance work).

## The method

### 1. Decode the job
Must-have skills, nice-to-haves, keywords, the problems this role solves, seniority signals.

### 2. Match
Map each must-have to the user's evidence. Flag gaps and how to address them honestly.

### 3. Resume
- **Header:** name, title matching the role, contact, LinkedIn or portfolio link.
- **Summary (3 lines):** who they are, top relevant strength, proof.
- **Experience:** reverse chronological, 3 to 6 bullets per role. Bullet formula: action verb + what + result (number, percentage, time saved, money, scale). "Grew a skincare brand's Instagram from 2,000 to 18,000 followers in 8 months, driving 40% of online sales."
- **Skills:** grouped, matching the job's keywords honestly.
- **Education and certifications.**
- **Freelance work:** framed as real experience with clients and results.
- **ATS layout:** simple, no tables or images in the main text, standard headings, 1 to 2 pages.

### 4. Cover letter (200 to 300 words)
1. Opening: the role and a strong reason (connection to their mission or a specific result).
2. Two short proof paragraphs matched to their top 2 needs.
3. Why this company.
4. Close with a confident next step.

### 5. Extras
LinkedIn headline and summary aligned with the application.

## Rules

- Never invent jobs, dates, degrees or numbers. If numbers are unknown, ask or use honest scope ("for 12 clients").
- Mirror keywords only where true.
- Remove irrelevant details, photos and personal data unless locally expected.

## Output format

```
WHAT THEY WANT
...
YOUR MATCH
| Requirement | Your evidence | Gap? |
RESUME
...
COVER LETTER
...
LINKEDIN HEADLINE
...
GAPS AND HOW TO HANDLE THEM
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Tailored to the job's must-haves
- [ ] Achievement bullets with results
- [ ] ATS-friendly
- [ ] Nothing invented

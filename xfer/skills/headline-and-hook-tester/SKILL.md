# Headline and Hook Tester

Opinions about headlines are cheap; a rubric makes the choice clear and teaches the user why. This skill scores each option, shows the weakness in plain words and produces stronger versions, then recommends what to test with real audiences.

## What to collect

1. The options (headlines, hooks, subject lines, titles, first lines).
2. Where they will be used (ad, email, Reel, YouTube, blog, landing page).
3. The audience and the payoff the content delivers.

## The rubric (1 to 5 each)

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Clarity | Is it instantly understood? |
| Specificity | Numbers, names, time frames, niches? |
| Curiosity | Does it create a gap the reader wants closed? |
| Benefit | Is the value or stakes for the reader obvious? |
| Relevance | Does the target audience feel it's for them? |
| Credibility | Is it believable? Does the content deliver it? |
| Fit | Right length and style for the platform? |

Total out of 35.

## The method

1. Score every option with a one-line reason per low score.
2. Identify patterns (all too vague, all too long, all about the sender).
3. Rewrite the bottom half using proven patterns: specific result, contrarian, mistake, how-to with a twist, question with tension, number, direct callout.
4. Produce 5 new options that beat the best original.
5. Recommend the top 2 to test and how (A/B email subject, two ad variants, YouTube test and compare, story poll).

## Rules

- Platform limits: email subject under 50 characters, YouTube title under 60, ad headline under 40, hook under 12 spoken words.
- Honest: no promise the content can't keep.
- Explain scores briefly and concretely.

## Output format

```
SCORES
| Option | Clarity | Specific | Curiosity | Benefit | Relevance | Credible | Fit | Total |

WHY THE TOP ONE WINS
...
WHAT'S HOLDING THE OTHERS BACK
...
REWRITES
...
5 NEW OPTIONS
...
TEST THESE TWO
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Every option scored with reasons
- [ ] Rewrites are measurably stronger
- [ ] Platform length respected
- [ ] Test recommendation given

# Template Adapter

Templates are written for one person in one niche. Swapping a few words leaves them sounding borrowed. This skill first works out why the template works, then rebuilds it for the user's world: their clients' problems, their language, their proof, their offer.

## What to collect

1. The template (paste it).
2. The user's profession, who they help, the result, how they deliver it.
3. Their clients' main problems and the words those clients use.
4. Real proof (results, numbers, testimonials) or none yet.
5. Channel and tone.

If the user only gives their job, ask 2 quick questions (who they help and the result). If they want speed, assume and label assumptions.

## The method

### 1. Reverse engineer the template
Label each part by its job: hook, rapport, problem, agitation, credibility, offer, proof, risk reversal, call to action. Note the psychology (curiosity, specificity, social proof, low-pressure ask).

### 2. Translate the substance
For each part, find the equivalent in the user's world:
- The problem the original names becomes the user's clients' problem.
- Examples, numbers and scenarios become ones from the user's niche.
- Jargon becomes the user's clients' language.
- Proof becomes the user's real proof, or is replaced with process or a free offer if there is none.
- The offer and ask fit what the user actually sells.

### 3. Keep the shape
Same order of jobs, similar length, same type of ask, same level of pressure.

### 4. Check it reads native
Would someone in the user's industry think a peer wrote it? Remove anything that only makes sense in the original niche.

### 5. Give variations
2 versions: one close to the original, one more personal or bolder.

## Rules

- Never copy proof or results from the original.
- Keep grammar right for the new subject (their/your, singular/plural).
- If part of the template doesn't fit the new niche, explain and replace it with something that does the same job.
- Mark details the user must fill in with [brackets].

## Output format

```
WHY THE ORIGINAL WORKS
1. [Hook] ... job: ...

ADAPTED VERSION
...

ALTERNATIVE (bolder / more personal)
...

WHAT I CHANGED AND WHY
...

FILL IN BEFORE USING
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Every part keeps its job
- [ ] No leftover details from the original niche
- [ ] Only the user's real proof
- [ ] Reads naturally in the new niche

# Scope of Work Writer

A scope protects both sides. It turns "make my Instagram better" into exact deliverables, dates and rules, so the client knows what they get and the freelancer knows when they are done. Vague scope is the root of most client conflict.

## What to collect

1. Project goal and context.
2. What the client asked for (paste notes or messages).
3. What the user will deliver and how.
4. Timeline, budget and payment structure.
5. Who on the client side approves work.

## The structure

1. **Project overview:** goal in one paragraph, success measures.
2. **Deliverables:** each with a description, quantity, format, specifications (sizes, length, platforms), and due date.
3. **Out of scope:** explicit list of what is not included (ad spend, extra platforms, photography, copywriting for ads, community management outside hours, new features).
4. **Client responsibilities:** content, access, approvals, feedback within X business days, brand assets.
5. **Timeline and milestones:** phases with dates, dependencies (a delay in client feedback moves the dates).
6. **Review and revisions:** number of revision rounds per deliverable, what counts as a revision versus a new request, how feedback is given (one consolidated document).
7. **Acceptance:** how a deliverable is approved, and that silence for X days counts as approval.
8. **Change requests:** how extra work is requested, quoted and approved before starting, at what rate.
9. **Payment terms:** amounts, dates, deposit, late fees, ownership transfer on full payment.
10. **Communication:** channel, response times, meeting rhythm.
11. **Sign off:** names, dates.

## Rules

- Measurable language: numbers, formats, dates. Replace "some", "a few", "ongoing", "as needed".
- Plain English. Short sentences.
- Not legal advice: recommend a lawyer for large or high-risk projects.
- Keep it fair. A one-sided scope damages trust.

## Output format

```
SCOPE OF WORK
Project: ... | Client: ... | Prepared by: ... | Date: ...

1. Overview
2. Deliverables
| # | Deliverable | Specs | Qty | Due |
3. Not included
4. Client responsibilities
5. Timeline
6. Revisions
7. Acceptance
8. Change requests
9. Payment
10. Communication
11. Sign off

VAGUE WORDS I REPLACED
"..." became "..."
```

## Final check

- [ ] Every deliverable has a number, format and date
- [ ] Out of scope list is explicit
- [ ] Revision and change request rules clear
- [ ] Client responsibilities stated

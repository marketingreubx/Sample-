# Landing Page Reviewer

Visitors decide in seconds whether a page is for them. This review looks at the page through the eyes of the buyer arriving from a specific ad, post or link, finds what makes them hesitate or leave, and ranks fixes by how much they will move conversions.

## What to collect

1. The page (link, screenshots desktop and mobile, or copy).
2. The goal (sign up, book, buy, message).
3. Traffic source (ad, Instagram bio, email, Google) and what the visitor saw before clicking.
4. Audience and offer.
5. Current numbers if available (visitors, conversion rate, bounce rate).

## The review

### 1. Five second test
After 5 seconds, can a visitor answer: What is this? Who is it for? What do I get? What do I do next? Write what a first-time visitor would think.

### 2. Message match
Does the headline match the promise of the ad or post that sent them?

### 3. Section by section
- **Headline and subheadline:** specific result, for whom, how.
- **Hero CTA:** visible without scrolling on mobile, clear verb.
- **Offer clarity:** what exactly is included, price or next step.
- **Proof:** testimonials, results, logos, numbers, close to claims.
- **Objections:** price, time, trust, fit answered.
- **Visuals:** support the message, real photos over stock.
- **Forms:** number of fields, friction, error states.
- **Trust:** contact details, policies, security, guarantee.
- **Mobile:** text size, button size, layout, sticky CTA.
- **Speed:** heavy images, popups (ask for PageSpeed scores if available).

### 4. Score
Out of 100 across clarity (25), relevance (15), proof (15), offer (15), friction (15), trust (15).

### 5. Fixes ranked
Impact (high, medium, low) and effort (quick, medium, big). Quick, high-impact first.

### 6. Rewrites
Headline (5 options), subheadline, CTA text, the weakest section rewritten.

### 7. Test ideas
2 to 3 A/B tests with hypothesis and metric.

## Rules

- Evidence for every point (quote what is on the page).
- Prioritise ruthlessly.
- No invented numbers.

## Output format

```
5 SECOND TEST
...
SCORE: [X]/100
| Area | Score | Notes |

TOP FIXES
| Fix | Why | Impact | Effort |

REWRITES
Headline: ...
Subheadline: ...
CTA: ...
Section: ...

TESTS
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Reviewed from the visitor's source and goal
- [ ] Every issue has evidence
- [ ] Fixes ranked by impact and effort
- [ ] Rewrites included

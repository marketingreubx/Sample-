# Networking Follow-Up Writer

Most networking dies after the event because the follow up is generic or never sent. A good follow up proves you listened, gives something useful, and suggests one small next step. It is sent within 24 hours.

## What to collect

1. Who they met, where, and what they talked about (any detail they remember: a problem, a project, a hobby, a book, their kids, a trip).
2. The relationship goal: potential client, referral partner, collaborator, mentor, friend in the industry.
3. Channel: email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, X.
4. Anything promised during the chat ("I'll send you that link").

## The method

### 1. Choose the value to give
Something tied to what they said: a resource, an introduction, an idea, a link to the thing mentioned, a short tip, a relevant post. Give before asking.

### 2. Write the first follow up (within 24 hours)
1. Reminder of where you met plus one specific detail from the chat.
2. The value (or the promised item).
3. One small next step or open door: "Would a 15 minute call next week be useful?" or simply "Let me know how the launch goes."
Keep it under 90 words. Warm, human, no pitch unless they asked.

### 3. Introductions
- Reply to the person who introduced you within the same day: thank them and move them to BCC.
- Then write to the new contact with context, why the intro made sense, and a simple next step with two time options.

### 4. Reconnecting with an old contact
Lead with something genuine (their recent post, a milestone, a memory), share a short update, no ask in the first message.

### 5. Keep in touch plan (30 days)
- Day 1: follow up.
- Day 7: comment on or share something of theirs.
- Day 14: send a relevant resource or intro.
- Day 30: check in or invite to the next step if it fits.

## Rules

- Specific details only. Never "Great to meet you at the event!" alone.
- No pitch to someone who did not ask, unless the conversation was explicitly about buying.
- If you promised something, deliver it in the first message.
- Match the channel: LinkedIn and email can be a bit longer, WhatsApp and DMs shorter.

## Output format

```
FOLLOW UP (send today)
Subject (if email): ...
...

IF THEY REPLY POSITIVELY
...

INTRODUCTION REPLY (if relevant)
To the introducer: ...
To the new contact: ...

KEEP IN TOUCH PLAN
Day 7: ... Day 14: ... Day 30: ...
```

## Final check

- [ ] References a real detail from the conversation
- [ ] Gives value before asking
- [ ] One small next step
- [ ] Under 90 words for the first message

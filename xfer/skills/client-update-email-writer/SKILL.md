# Client Update Email Writer

Silence makes clients nervous. Short, regular updates make them relaxed and loyal, and they prevent most conflicts. Bad news delivered early, with a plan, builds more trust than good news delivered late.

## What to collect

1. The project and what happened since the last update.
2. Type of update: weekly status, milestone, delay, problem or mistake, waiting on the client, completion.
3. Dates: deadlines, new dates if delayed.
4. What is needed from the client.
5. Channel and tone.

## Formats

### Weekly status
```
Subject: [Project] update, week of [date]
Done this week: (3 bullets max)
Next week: (3 bullets)
Need from you: (with a date)
Risks: (only if real)
```

### Milestone reached
Celebrate the result with a number or link, what it means for their goal, what comes next.

### Delay notice
1. Say it in the first line. No warm-up.
2. Short reason (no excuses, no blaming).
3. Impact: what moves and the new date.
4. What you are doing to protect quality or catch up.
5. Option if any (partial delivery, priority change).
Send as soon as the delay is known.

### Problem or mistake
1. What happened, plainly.
2. What you already did to fix or contain it.
3. What happens next and when.
4. How you will prevent it.
5. One apology, sincere and short.

### Waiting on the client
Friendly, specific about what is needed, why it matters for the timeline, and the date after which dates move. Offer to make it easy (a template, a quick call).

### Completion
What was delivered, results, where to find files, next steps.

Each update comes in an email version and a short chat version.

## Rules

- Lead with the most important line.
- Short. Bullets. Under 150 words for routine updates.
- Dates, not "soon".
- Own mistakes without over-apologising.
- Never hide bad news in the middle.

## Output format

```
EMAIL
Subject: ...
...

WHATSAPP / SLACK VERSION
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Main point in the first line
- [ ] Specific dates
- [ ] Clear ask from the client if needed
- [ ] Short enough to read in 30 seconds

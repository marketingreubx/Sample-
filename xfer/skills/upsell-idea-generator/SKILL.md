# Upsell Idea Generator

The easiest sale is to a happy client. A good upsell is the next problem the client will face after the current one is solved, offered right after a win. It should feel like advice, not a pitch.

## What to collect

1. The client, what the user currently delivers, price, and how long they have worked together.
2. Results so far and anything the client has mentioned wanting.
3. The client's business goals for the next 3 to 12 months.
4. The user's other skills or partners they could bring in.

## The method

### 1. Map the client's journey
What comes after the current result? Examples: more followers leads to needing a booking system; more bookings leads to needing staff training or retention emails; a new website leads to needing SEO and ads.

### 2. Generate 8 to 12 ideas in four types
- **Upgrade:** more of the same (more posts, more platforms, faster turnaround).
- **Add on:** a related service (email marketing, ads management, photography day, Google profile).
- **Next stage:** the next problem in their journey (funnel, retention, launches).
- **Premium:** strategy, training, done-with-you workshops, priority support.

### 3. Score each (1 to 5)
Value to the client, connection to their stated goals, ease for the user to deliver, profit for the user, evidence the client wants it. Pick the top 2.

### 4. Price and package
Price each top idea as a monthly add on or a fixed project, with a bundle discount only if the user wants it.

### 5. Timing
Offer right after a visible win, in a monthly report, or in a quarterly review. Never during a problem.

### 6. The message
1. Celebrate the win with numbers.
2. Name the next opportunity or risk you see.
3. Suggest the solution briefly.
4. Easy next step: "Want me to put together a quick plan?"

## Rules

- Only suggest things that genuinely help the client.
- One upsell at a time.
- Never undermine current work to create a need.
- Real numbers only.

## Output format

```
CLIENT JOURNEY
Now: ... Next: ... After that: ...

IDEAS (scored)
| Idea | Type | Value | Fit | Ease | Profit | Evidence | Total |

TOP 2
1. ... Price: ... Why now: ...
2. ...

WHEN TO OFFER
...

MESSAGE
...

IF THEY SAY "NOT NOW"
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Ideas tied to the client's goals
- [ ] Offered after a win
- [ ] Message leads with their result
- [ ] Priced clearly

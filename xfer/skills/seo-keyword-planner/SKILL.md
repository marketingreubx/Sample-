# SEO Keyword Planner

SEO works when every important search the buyer makes has one clear page that answers it best. This skill builds that map from the business's real services and customers, prioritises what can rank soonest, and gives a 90 day plan. Search volumes are estimates unless the user provides tool data.

## What to collect

1. Business, services, locations served, and who buys.
2. Website URL and current pages (or a list).
3. Competitors that rank well.
4. Keyword tool data if available (Google Search Console, Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush, Ubersuggest).
5. Goal: leads, bookings, product sales, authority.

## The method

### 1. Seed topics
From services, problems customers describe, questions they ask on calls, and competitor pages.

### 2. Expand into keyword groups
For each seed: main term, variations, long tail questions, local modifiers ("near me", city, area), comparison terms ("X vs Y"), price terms ("cost of", "how much").

### 3. Classify by intent
- **Transactional / local:** ready to buy or book ("facial clinic in Lekki").
- **Commercial:** comparing ("best skincare clinic Lagos", "X vs Y").
- **Informational:** learning ("how to treat hyperpigmentation").

### 4. Map to pages
One primary keyword group per page:
- Service pages for transactional terms.
- Location pages only if real locations or service areas.
- Blog posts and guides for informational terms, linking to service pages.
- FAQ sections for questions.
Flag cannibalisation (two pages targeting the same thing).

### 5. Prioritise
Score: business value, ranking difficulty (estimate), and current position if Search Console data exists. Quick wins first: pages already ranking 8 to 20, local terms, low-competition questions.

### 6. Local SEO actions
Google Business Profile categories, services, posts, photos, reviews plan, consistent name, address and phone across listings.

### 7. 90 day plan
Weeks 1 to 4: fix and optimise existing pages. Weeks 5 to 8: new service or location pages. Weeks 9 to 12: supporting articles and links.

## Rules

- Label all volumes and difficulty as estimates unless from a tool.
- Never recommend keyword stuffing, fake locations or spammy links.
- Write page titles and H1s for humans.

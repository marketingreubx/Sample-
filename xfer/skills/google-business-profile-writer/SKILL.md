# Google Business Profile Writer

For local businesses, the Google profile is often the first impression and the main source of calls and bookings. Rankings depend on relevance, distance and prominence. This skill improves what the user controls: complete, accurate, keyword-relevant information, fresh posts and photos, and a steady flow of reviews.

## What to collect

1. Business name (exactly as on signage), address or service area, phone, website, hours.
2. Services with prices or price ranges, and products.
3. What customers search for (in their words) and the main areas served.
4. What makes the business different and any proof.
5. Current reviews (paste a few) and rating.

## The method

### 1. Categories
Recommend the most specific primary category and 2 to 5 relevant secondary categories.

### 2. Business description (up to 750 characters)
Who you serve, main services, what makes you different, location relevance, a soft call to action. Natural keywords, no stuffing, no URLs or promotions (not allowed).

### 3. Services
Each service with a 1 to 2 sentence description (up to 300 characters) and price where possible.

### 4. Products (if relevant)
Name, description, price, link.

### 5. Q&A
5 to 8 common questions and answers to post.

### 6. Posts (monthly plan)
4 posts a month: an offer, an update or tip, a result or testimonial, an event or seasonal post. Each with copy (100 to 300 words), photo idea and button.

### 7. Photos
What to upload: exterior, interior, team at work, before and after (where allowed), products, at least 5 new photos a month.

### 8. Reviews
Review request message (WhatsApp and SMS) with the direct review link, timing (right after a great experience), and replies to positive, neutral and negative reviews.

### 9. Consistency
Make sure name, address and phone match across the website, Instagram, directories.

## Rules

- Follow Google's guidelines: no keyword stuffing in the business name, no fake reviews, no incentives for reviews, accurate address.
- Real information only.

# Monthly Client Report Writer

Clients do not read dashboards. They read the first three lines and look for: did it work, why, and what is next. A good report answers those in plain language, connects numbers to the client's goal, and makes renewing easy.

## What to collect

1. The client's goals and the key metrics that matter for them (bookings, leads, sales, reach, followers, engagement, website visits, ad cost per result).
2. This month's numbers, last month's numbers, and targets (paste or screenshot).
3. What was done this month.
4. Notable events (a viral post, a holiday, a platform change, a client delay).

## The method

### 1. Headline
One sentence with the most important result: "Instagram brought 18 booking inquiries this month, up from 11, mostly from Reels."

### 2. Key numbers table
Metric, this month, last month, change (%), goal, status. 4 to 7 metrics maximum, business metrics first, vanity metrics last.

### 3. What worked and why
2 to 3 points with evidence (the top post, the best ad, the change that caused it).

### 4. What didn't and what we're changing
Honest, with the fix.

### 5. Top content or activity
Top 3 items with numbers and a one-line reason each.

### 6. Next month's plan
3 priorities linked to the goal, with expected impact stated conservatively.

### 7. Decisions or input needed from the client
Approvals, content, budget changes, with dates.

### 8. The email that sends it
3 to 5 lines with the headline result and a link.

## Interpretation rules

- Calculate changes correctly and show the formula if unclear. Double check percentages.
- Explain jargon in brackets the first time (reach: number of different people who saw it).
- Do not claim causation without evidence. Say "likely" when inferring.
- Small numbers need care: from 2 to 4 is +100% but still small. Say so.
- Never invent missing numbers. Mark them as "not available".

## Output format

```
[CLIENT] MONTHLY REPORT, [MONTH]

THE HEADLINE
...

KEY NUMBERS
| Metric | This month | Last month | Change | Goal | Status |

WHAT WORKED
...
WHAT WE'RE CHANGING
...

...
NEXT MONTH
...

...

...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Headline answers "did it work?"
- [ ] Math checked
- [ ] Business metrics first
- [ ] Clear next steps and decisions

# Interview Prep Coach

Interviews reward preparation, not talent at improvising. The user needs 6 to 8 strong stories from their real experience that can answer most questions, a clear "why me, why this", and practice saying it out loud. This skill builds that, then rehearses.

## What to collect

1. The job description and company.
2. The user's CV or experience.
3. Interview format (phone, video, panel, technical test, case study) and stage.
4. Their worries (gaps, nerves, salary, weak areas).

## The method

### 1. What they're looking for
Top 5 qualities and skills from the job description, and the problems the role solves.

### 2. Story bank (STAR)
6 to 8 stories from real experience: Situation, Task, Action (what the user did), Result (with numbers). Each tagged with the questions it answers (leadership, conflict, failure, success, pressure, teamwork, initiative).

### 3. Core answers
- "Tell me about yourself" (60 to 90 seconds: present, past, why this role).
- "Why this company / role?"
- "Strengths" and "weakness" (real, with improvement).
- "Where do you see yourself?"
- Salary expectations (researched range, how to phrase).
- Gaps or career changes (honest, brief, forward-looking).

### 4. Likely questions
15 to 20 predicted questions with outline answers pointing to the story bank.

### 5. Questions to ask them
5 thoughtful questions about success in the role, team, challenges.

### 6. Mock interview
Ask one question at a time and wait. After each answer, feedback: what worked, what to tighten, a stronger version. End with a score on structure, relevance, evidence, confidence and length.

### 7. Logistics
Day-before checklist, video setup tips, follow up thank-you message.

## Rules

- Only the user's real experience.
- Answers 1 to 2 minutes, structured.
- Encouraging but honest feedback.

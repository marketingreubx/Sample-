# Email Reply Writer

A good reply answers what was asked, says what needs saying, sets the next step and sounds like a calm, competent person. It is usually shorter than the user expects.

## What to collect

1. The message received (paste the whole thread if relevant).
2. What the user wants to achieve (agree, decline, delay, ask, push back, apologise, close).
3. Facts the reply must include (dates, prices, decisions).
4. Relationship and tone (client, boss, colleague, stranger; warm, neutral, firm).
5. Channel (email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Slack).

## The method

### 1. Read the message
What exactly are they asking? What do they feel? What do they need to hear? What is the deadline?

### 2. Decide the outcome
The one thing the reply must achieve, and the next step.

### 3. Write the reply
1. Greeting by name.
2. First line answers the main question or states the decision.
3. Context or reason (brief).
4. Any detail they need (dates, options, links).
5. The next step or question.
6. Sign off.

### 4. Hard situations (patterns)
- **Saying no:** thank, decline clearly, short reason (optional), alternative if possible.
- **Apology:** own it, impact, fix, prevention. One apology, not five.
- **Complaint received:** acknowledge, take responsibility for your part, solution, timeline.
- **Rescheduling:** apologise briefly, two new options.
- **Chasing a reply:** short, specific, easy to answer.
- **Rude message:** stay factual, respond only to the substance.
- **Asking for something:** context, the ask, deadline, why it helps them too.

### 5. Two versions
Short (2 to 4 lines) and fuller (with context). Subject line options for new email threads.

## Rules

- Answer the question first.
- Match their level of formality.
- No over-apologising, no passive aggression, no corporate filler ("per my last email", "I hope this email finds you well" unless the user wants it).
- Never invent facts or commitments.

## Output format

```
WHAT THEY'RE REALLY ASKING
...

REPLY (short)
...

REPLY (fuller)
...

SUBJECT LINE (if needed)
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Main question answered in the first lines
- [ ] Clear next step
- [ ] Tone matches the relationship
- [ ] No invented commitments

# Referral Request Writer

Happy clients want to help but do not know how. Referrals happen when the user asks at the right moment, makes it specific ("who else do you know who..."), and gives the client a ready message to forward. This skill builds that whole loop.

## What to collect

1. The service, the ideal referred client, and the result clients get.
2. The client relationship: current, finished, how happy, any recent win.
3. Channel: WhatsApp, email, DM, in person.
4. Whether the user wants to offer a reward (and what they can afford).

## The method

### 1. Pick the moment
Ask right after a win: a great result, a compliment from the client, a renewal, a project delivered on time, a positive review. Never in the middle of a problem.

### 2. Make the ask specific
Instead of "Do you know anyone?", describe the person: "Do you know another studio owner who's fully booked on weekends but quiet on weekdays?" Specific descriptions trigger names.

### 3. Write the messages
- **The ask** (after a win): thank them for something specific, mention the result, describe the ideal referral, ask if anyone comes to mind, and offer to make it easy.
- **The forwardable intro:** a short message the client can copy and send to their friend, written in the client's voice, with the user's link or number.
- **The reward mention (optional):** a thank you that fits the relationship (a discount, a free month, a gift, a donation). Check local rules and keep it honest.
- **Thank you note:** sent the same day a referral arrives, whether or not it converts.
- **Follow up to the referred person:** within 24 hours, mention who introduced them.

### 4. Make it a habit
- Add a referral line to the offboarding and quarterly review messages.
- Monthly reminder to ask 3 happy clients.
- Track referrals in a simple sheet: who referred, who, date, outcome, thank you sent.

## Rules

- One ask per milestone. Never pester.
- Do not guilt or pressure. Make it easy to say "no one comes to mind right now".
- Never promise rewards you might not give.
- Personalise every ask with a real detail from the project.

## Output format

```
WHEN TO ASK (for this client)
...

THE ASK
...

FORWARDABLE INTRO (for them to send)
...

IF THEY SAY "LET ME THINK"
...

THANK YOU NOTE (when a referral arrives)
...

FIRST MESSAGE TO THE REFERRED PERSON
...

REWARD (optional)
...

MAKE IT A HABIT
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Asked after a real win
- [ ] Describes a specific type of person
- [ ] Includes a ready-to-forward message
- [ ] Easy for the client to say no

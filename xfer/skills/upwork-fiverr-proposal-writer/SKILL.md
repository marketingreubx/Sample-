# Marketplace Proposal Writer

Clients on marketplaces read the first 2 lines in the preview and skip the rest if it sounds copied. A winning proposal opens with their problem, proves relevance fast, and ends with a question that makes them reply. Short wins.

## What to collect

1. The full job post (and client history if visible: hire rate, spend, reviews).
2. The user's relevant experience, 1 to 3 portfolio links and results.
3. Their rate or bid range.
4. Any screening questions.

## The method

### 1. Decode the post
- The stated task.
- The real goal behind it (what success means for the client's business).
- Red flags (vague scope, very low budget, unpaid test work, off-platform payment requests). Warn the user.
- Keywords and any hidden instruction (some posts ask you to start with a word to prove you read it).

### 2. Write the opening 2 lines
They show in the preview. Restate their goal or problem in their own words and hint at the solution. Never start with "Hi, my name is" or "I am a professional with 10 years of experience".

### 3. Body (80 to 160 words)
1. One relevant example with a result and a link.
2. How you would approach their project in 2 to 4 short steps, with a smart observation they did not mention.
3. Timeline and what they get.
4. A question about their project that invites a reply.

### 4. Bid and screening
Suggest a bid with reasoning (value, scope, market), milestones for larger jobs, and answer screening questions directly and briefly.

### 5. Follow up
A short message if no reply in 3 days, adding one new useful idea.

### 6. Fiverr variant
For buyer requests or gig messages: even shorter, and point to the right gig package.

## Rules

- Proof over adjectives. One relevant sample beats five unrelated ones.
- Use their words from the post.
- No copy pasted templates, no flattery, no "Dear Sir/Madam".
- Never invent experience or results.
- Respect platform rules: no contact details or off-platform payment.

## Output format

```
WHAT THEY REALLY WANT
...
RED FLAGS (if any)
...

PROPOSAL
[first 2 lines]
...

SUGGESTED BID
... because ...

SCREENING ANSWERS
1. ...

FOLLOW UP (day 3)
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] First 2 lines are about the client's goal
- [ ] One relevant example with a result
- [ ] Ends with a question
- [ ] Under 200 words
- [ ] No platform rule violations

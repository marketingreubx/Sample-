# Pricing Strategist

Price is a positioning decision, not a math problem alone. This skill finds the floor (what the user must charge to run a healthy business), checks the market range, then prices toward the value created, and packages it so the buyer chooses between options instead of deciding whether to buy.

## What to collect

1. What the user sells, to whom, and the result it creates for the client.
2. Time and costs to deliver one client (hours, tools, contractors).
3. Income goal and capacity (how many clients they can serve well).
4. Current prices and how clients react.
5. Competitor or market prices they know.
6. Currency and market (local versus international clients).

## The method

### 1. The floor
(Monthly income goal + business costs + tax set aside) ÷ realistic number of clients = minimum price per client. Show the math. Below this, the business does not work.

### 2. The market
Typical low, middle and high prices for this service in this market, based on what the user provides. Label it as an estimate if the user gives no data.

### 3. The value
What the result is worth to the client: extra revenue, time saved, cost avoided. Example: if the client's average booking is 25,000 and the service adds 15 bookings a month, the value is 375,000 a month. A price of 10% to 30% of the value created is easy to justify.

### 4. Choose the position
Budget, middle or premium, based on the user's proof, niche and capacity. Premium requires a specific niche, strong proof and a clear process.

### 5. Package (good, better, best)
- **Starter:** the core result, limited scope. Exists to make the middle look good.
- **Growth (the one most should buy):** the full result.
- **Premium:** done-for-you plus extras, priority, strategy.
Each package names the outcome, deliverables, frequency, and what is not included. Price the middle at the target. Price the top at 2 to 3 times the middle.

### 6. Present it
- Recap the client's goal and the cost of the problem first.
- Show 3 options, recommend one, then stop talking.
- Script for "that's more than I expected".
- Payment terms: upfront, deposit, monthly, minimum term.

### 7. Raise prices plan
New clients get new prices now. Existing clients get 30 days' notice, with a thank you and a reason. Raise again after every 3 to 5 new clients at the current price.

## Rules

- Never price below the floor without a clear reason (for example a time limited founding client offer for a testimonial).
- Do not discount. Remove scope instead.
- Odd prices (97, 297) suit products. Round prices (300,000 or 1,500) suit services.
- Show local and international pricing separately if both apply.

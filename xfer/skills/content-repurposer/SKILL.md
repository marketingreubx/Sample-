# Content Repurposer

One strong idea deserves more than one post. Repurposing is not copying the same text everywhere; it is finding the 5 to 10 best moments in a long piece and rebuilding each one for how people consume that platform.

## What to collect

1. The source (transcript, article, notes, recording timestamps).
2. Platforms the user posts on and formats they can make.
3. Audience and goal (reach, leads, authority, sales).
4. The call to action for this content cycle.
5. Brand voice samples if available.

## The method

### 1. Mine the source
Pull out: key ideas, stories, surprising statements, frameworks, numbers, mistakes, quotes, questions answered. Label each with a timestamp or paragraph reference.

### 2. Pick the best 8 to 12 atoms
Score by: strong hook potential, standalone value, fit with the audience, fit with the CTA.

### 3. Rebuild per platform
- **Short video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts):** clip start and end time or a re-recorded script, hook, on-screen text, caption.
- **Carousel:** cover, 6 to 8 slides, final CTA slide, caption.
- **LinkedIn post:** story or lesson format.
- **X or Threads:** a short post and a thread.
- **Instagram Stories:** a 4 to 5 frame sequence with a poll or question sticker.
- **Newsletter:** one issue built around the main idea.
- **Quote graphics:** 3 quotes.
- **YouTube community or Facebook post:** if relevant.

### 4. Schedule
A 1 to 4 week plan spreading the pieces across platforms, with the original long piece linked where useful.

## Rules

- Each piece must stand alone without the original.
- Native to the platform: length, tone and format.
- Quotes exact when attributed.
- Do not post identical text across platforms.

## Output format

```
BEST MOMENTS
| # | Idea | Source time | Hook potential |

CONTENT PACK
Reel 1 (clip 03:12 to 03:58): hook ..., text ..., caption ...
Carousel 1: slides ...
LinkedIn post: ...
X thread: ...
Stories: ...
Newsletter: ...
Quotes: ...

SCHEDULE
| Day | Platform | Piece |
```

## Final check

- [ ] 8 to 12 distinct pieces
- [ ] Each adapted to its platform
- [ ] Each works on its own
- [ ] Schedule included

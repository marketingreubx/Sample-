# Guarantee and Risk Reversal Writer

People do not buy when the risk feels like theirs alone. A good guarantee moves some risk to the seller in a way the seller can actually honour. A bad one is vague, unrealistic or attracts people who want something for free.

## What to collect

1. The offer, price, and how results happen (what the client must do too).
2. What the seller can control and what they cannot.
3. Past results and refund history if any.
4. The buyer's biggest fear (wasting money, no results, being stuck in a contract, poor service).
5. Any legal or platform rules that apply.

## Guarantee types (choose and explain)

1. **Unconditional:** "Not happy in 14 days, full refund." Strongest for low price, low cost to deliver.
2. **Conditional (do the work):** refund if the client did the agreed actions and did not get the stated result. Fits coaching and courses.
3. **Performance / work until:** "If you don't get X by Y, we keep working for free until you do." Fits services where the seller controls most of the outcome.
4. **Service level:** promises about the process, not the result. "Posts delivered every Monday or that week is free." Fits most freelancers.
5. **Pilot or trial:** a small paid first step with no long contract. "First month at a fixed scope, cancel anytime."
6. **Implementation:** "If you can't set it up, we'll do it with you on a call."
7. **Anti-guarantee:** no refunds, explained honestly (digital access, custom work) plus reassurance through support or quality.

## Writing the guarantee

- **Name it** (optional): "The Booked Calendar Promise".
- **The promise:** what exactly is guaranteed, in one or two sentences.
- **The time frame.**
- **The conditions:** what the client must do, in plain words, few and fair.
- **The remedy:** refund, free extra work, credit.
- **How to claim:** simple steps.
- **Why we offer it:** one line that builds confidence.

## Where to use it

Sales page (near the price), proposal (investment section), call script (after the price), checkout page, FAQ.

## Rules

- Only promise what the seller controls or can afford to honour.
- Conditions must be measurable and fair. No hidden traps.
- Never guarantee specific income or medical results.
- Recommend checking local consumer law and platform policies.

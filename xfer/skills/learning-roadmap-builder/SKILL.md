# Learning Roadmap Builder

Most people stall when learning a skill because they consume tutorials without a target, practice or proof. This roadmap starts with a concrete goal ("good enough to get paid for short-form editing in 12 weeks"), then builds the skill in stages through projects that double as a portfolio.

## What to collect

1. The skill and why (job, clients, hobby, own business).
2. Target level and deadline.
3. Current level and related skills.
4. Hours per week and budget for tools or courses.
5. How they like to learn.

## The method

### 1. Define the target
What the user will be able to do at the end, in concrete terms, and how it will be proven (a portfolio, a client project, a certificate, a test).

### 2. Break the skill down
Sub-skills grouped into 3 to 5 stages (foundations, core, intermediate, specialisation, professional). For each sub-skill: why it matters.

### 3. Weekly plan
For each week: learning goal, what to study (resource types: official docs, a course, YouTube, books, templates), practice drills, and time needed. 70% practice, 30% input.

### 4. Projects
One project per stage, increasing in difficulty, ending with real or realistic client work. Each project has a brief and a "done" checklist.

### 5. Checkpoints
Self-test or feedback point at the end of each stage (compare to professional examples, get feedback from a community, a mini test).

### 6. Routine
Daily or weekly routine that fits the hours available, plus how to stay consistent (tracking, accountability).

### 7. Getting paid (if relevant)
When and how to start offering the skill: first offer, portfolio, where to find first clients.

## Rules

- Realistic for the hours available.
- Practice-heavy.
- Recommend resource types and well-known free options; never invent course names or links.

## Output format

```

```

## Final check

- [ ] Concrete target and proof
- [ ] Weekly plan fits available time
- [ ] Projects build a portfolio
- [ ] Checkpoints included

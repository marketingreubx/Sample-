# Course Outline Builder

People buy courses for a transformation, not information. A good course takes students from a clear starting point to a clear result through the shortest path, with practice at every step and an early win that keeps them going.

## What to collect

1. Topic and the user's expertise and results.
2. The student: who they are, their starting level, their goal.
3. The promised transformation and time frame.
4. Format preferences (pre-recorded, live cohort, workshop, hybrid) and platform.
5. Price idea and launch timing.

## The method

### 1. Transformation
"By the end, you will [result] even if [obstacle]." Define before and after states.

### 2. Map the path
List every step a student must take to get the result, in order. Remove anything not essential (nice-to-know goes to bonuses).

### 3. Modules and lessons
Group steps into 4 to 8 modules. For each module: outcome, lessons (5 to 15 minutes each), an action or assignment, a template or tool, a checkpoint.

### 4. Quick win
Lesson 1 or 2 delivers a visible result within 24 hours.

### 5. Support and community
Q&A calls, feedback on assignments, a group chat, office hours, progress tracking.

### 6. Pilot
Run a beta with 5 to 20 students at a founding price, gather feedback and testimonials, then improve.

### 7. Pricing and launch
Price options linked to the transformation, payment plans, a simple launch plan (waitlist, free training, open cart, close).

## Rules

- Outcome first, content second.
- Every lesson ends with an action.
- Avoid overwhelming students; less but complete.
- Honest promises; disclaimers for income-related outcomes.

- [ ] Clear transformation
- [ ] Logical path, no filler
- [ ] Action in every lesson
- [ ] Early quick win
- [ ] Pilot plan

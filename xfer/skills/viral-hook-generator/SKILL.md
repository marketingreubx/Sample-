# Viral Hook Generator

The hook decides whether anything else gets seen. It has to create a gap the viewer needs to close, in under 2 seconds, in words, text and visuals at the same time. This skill writes hooks that match the content honestly (no bait the video cannot pay off) and are specific to the viewer.

## What to collect

1. Topic and the payoff of the content (what the viewer gets by the end).
2. Audience: who, their level, what they want, what they fear.
3. Platform and format (talking head, voiceover, text on screen, carousel cover, ad).
4. The creator's tone (calm expert, bold, funny, relatable).
5. Any proof they can use (numbers, results, credentials).

## The 15 hook patterns

1. **Specific result:** "How I got 21 bookings from one Reel."
2. **Contrarian:** "Posting every day is why you're not getting clients."
3. **Mistake callout:** "Stop putting your prices in your bio."
4. **Direct callout:** "If you're an esthetician with empty weekdays, watch this."
5. **Curiosity gap:** "The one setting that doubled my reach."
6. **Before and after:** "Day 1 vs day 90 of doing this."
7. **Question with tension:** "Why do clients ghost after you send the price?"
8. **Number list promise:** "3 captions that get DMs."
9. **Story in the middle:** "She cancelled 10 minutes before the appointment, and it was the best thing that happened."
10. **Myth vs truth:** "You don't need more followers. You need this."
11. **Secret or insider:** "What agencies don't tell you about retainers."
12. **Challenge:** "Try this on your next post and tell me what happens."
13. **Warning:** "Don't sign a client before you ask this question."
14. **Relatable moment:** "When the client says 'just one more small change'..."
15. **Shock statistic (true only):** "80% of your followers never see your posts."

## The method

1. Write 15 hooks, one per pattern, for the user's topic.
2. For each: spoken line (under 12 words), on-screen text (under 7 words), visual or first frame idea.
3. Score each 1 to 5 on: specificity, curiosity, relevance to the audience, honesty (payoff matches), and ease to film. Rank them.
4. Rewrite the top 3 into 3 variations each (different word order or angle).
5. Write a re-hook for the 3 to 5 second mark ("But here's the part nobody talks about...") and a pattern interrupt idea (cut, zoom, prop).
6. For carousels: write the cover line plus slide 2 line that pays it off.

## Rules

- The content must deliver what the hook promises.
- Specific beats general: numbers, names, niches, time frames.
- Front-load the most important word.
- No fake statistics or fake results.
- Under 12 spoken words. Text on screen readable in one glance.
- Avoid overused openers ("Hey guys", "In today's video", "Did you know").

# Elevator Pitch Builder

A pitch is not a summary of your services. It is a short story that makes the listener curious enough to ask the next question. The best pitches name who you help, the result they get, and one detail that makes it real, in words a 12 year old understands.

## What to collect

1. What the user does and for whom.
2. The result clients get, ideally with one real example or number.
3. What makes their way different (method, niche, speed, guarantee, background).
4. The setting: networking event, sales call opener, interview, podcast intro, social bio, video intro.
5. The usual listener: potential clients, referral partners, investors, peers.

## The method

### 1. Find the core line
Fill in: "I help [specific who] [get specific result] [without / through / in specific way]."
Write 5 versions, then choose the one with the clearest who and the most concrete result.

### 2. Add the curiosity gap
Add a detail that makes people want to ask: an unusual method, a surprising number, a specific niche. "I help solo estheticians fill their weekday gaps using only Instagram Stories" makes people ask "only Stories?"

### 3. Build three lengths
- **10 seconds:** the core line only.
- **30 seconds:** problem they face, what you do, one result, a question back.
- **60 seconds:** a mini story: one client, their situation before, what you did, what changed, and an invitation.

### 4. End with a turn
Every pitch ends by handing the conversation back: "What about you?" or "Do you know anyone struggling with that?" Pitches that end with a statement end the conversation.

### 5. Adapt
- Bio version (under 150 characters).
- Answer for "So what do you do?" at a party (casual, no jargon).
- Podcast or live intro (third person).
- LinkedIn headline.

## Rules

- No jargon: "omnichannel", "solutions", "leverage", "synergy", "digital transformation".
- Specific beats impressive. "Bookings" beats "growth". "Skincare clinics" beats "businesses".
- Only true results. If there are none yet, use the process or the problem instead.
- Must sound natural spoken out loud. Read it aloud test: no sentence longer than one breath.

```
Say the 30 second version out loud 5 times today. Change any word that trips you.
```

## Final check

- [ ] Names a specific who and a concrete result
- [ ] Contains one detail that sparks a question
- [ ] Ends by handing the conversation back
- [ ] Reads naturally out loud

# Blog Post Writer

Good blog posts answer the reader's question better than anything else on the first page of Google, show real experience, and lead naturally to the business. This skill writes for people first: clear, specific, scannable, with search optimisation built in, not stuffed in.

## What to collect

1. Topic or target keyword, and the reader (who and their level).
2. The business and its offer (for the call to action).
3. First-hand experience, examples, data, opinions to include.
4. Desired length and tone.
5. Existing pages to link to.

## The method

### 1. Search intent
What does the reader want: to learn, compare, buy, fix? Choose the format that fits (how-to, list, guide, comparison, case study, opinion).

### 2. Outline
Title, H2s and H3s that cover the topic fully, in logical order, including the questions people also ask.

### 3. Opening (first 100 words)
Answer the main question directly or state the promise, then why to trust this article (experience), then what is covered.

### 4. Body
- One idea per section, short paragraphs (2 to 4 lines).
- Steps numbered, examples concrete, real numbers.
- Tables, checklists or templates where useful.
- First-hand details: "When we tested this with 12 clients..." (only if true).
- Mention mistakes and edge cases.

### 5. Ending
Summary in 3 bullets, then a natural call to action tied to the topic (lead magnet, service, booking).

### 6. SEO pack
Meta title (under 60 characters), meta description (under 155), URL slug, 3 to 5 internal link suggestions with anchor text, image ideas with alt text, FAQ section with 3 to 5 questions.

## Rules

- Clarity over keyword density. Use the keyword naturally in the title, first paragraph and a heading.
- No filler intros ("In today's fast-paced world").
- No invented statistics. Mark claims that need a source with [source].
- Reading level: clear to a smart 14 year old.
- No em dashes.

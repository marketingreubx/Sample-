# Sales Page Copywriter

A sales page is a conversation with one buyer who has questions in a predictable order: Is this for me? Do I believe the problem? Why hasn't anything worked? What is this? Will it work for me? What do I get? What does it cost? What if it doesn't work? Why now? This skill answers each question in that order, in the buyer's words.

## What to collect

1. The offer: what it is, format, what is included, price, payment plans, delivery.
2. The buyer: who, their pain, desire, objections, awareness level (unaware, problem aware, solution aware, product aware).
3. The result and the mechanism (the specific way the offer gets the result).
4. Proof: testimonials, results, numbers, case studies, the seller's story.
5. Guarantee, bonuses, deadline (only if real).
6. Traffic source: cold ads, warm audience, email list. This decides length and tone.

## Page structure

1. **Headline:** the specific result for the specific person, plus the mechanism or time frame. Give 5 options.
2. **Subheadline:** removes the biggest doubt.
3. **Hero call to action** and one line of proof.
4. **Lead (call out):** "If you're a ... who ... this is for you." Mirror their situation.
5. **The problem:** what is happening, what it costs, why it is not their fault.
6. **Why the usual fixes fail:** the false beliefs, and the real cause.
7. **The story:** the seller's or a client's turning point (short, true).
8. **The mechanism:** the named method, why it works, 3 to 5 pillars.
9. **The offer:** what they get, each part framed as a benefit and outcome.
10. **Value stack:** list each component with honest value framing. Bonuses that remove objections.
11. **Proof:** testimonials matched to objections, results with context.
12. **Price reveal:** anchor against the cost of the problem or alternatives, then the price and payment options.
13. **Guarantee:** clear, specific, honest.
14. **Who it is not for:** builds trust.
15. **FAQ:** 6 to 10 real objections answered.
16. **Urgency:** only real reasons (cohort start date, price increase, limited spots).
17. **Final call to action** with a recap.
18. **P.S.:** restate the core promise and deadline.

## Rules

- Write to one person, in second person, at a 6th to 8th grade reading level.
- Specific beats vague: numbers, names, time frames.
- Benefits over features: every feature followed by "so you can...".
- No fake scarcity, fake countdowns, invented testimonials or unrealistic income claims. Add a results disclaimer where appropriate.
- Calls to action every 2 to 3 sections, same wording.
- Short paragraphs, scannable subheads, bullets.
- Match length to awareness: colder traffic needs more story and proof.

## Output format

```
HEADLINE OPTIONS (5)
...
[SECTION 1: HERO]
Copy: ...
Design note: ...
[SECTION 2: ...]
...
FAQ
...
CTA BUTTON TEXT OPTIONS
...
WHAT TO ADD BEFORE PUBLISHING
- [proof needed], [screenshots], [legal disclaimer]
```

## Final check

- [ ] Answers the buyer's questions in order
- [ ] Mechanism is named and explained
- [ ] Every claim backed by provided proof
- [ ] Guarantee and urgency are real
- [ ] CTA repeated through the page

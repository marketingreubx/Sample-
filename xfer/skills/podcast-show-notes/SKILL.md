# Podcast Show Notes

Show notes do three jobs: help new listeners decide to press play, help search engines understand the episode, and give the creator ready-made content to promote it. Everything comes from the transcript, accurately.

## What to collect

1. The transcript (with timestamps if available).
2. Show name, host, guest name and links.
3. Audience and the show's tone.
4. Platforms for promotion.
5. Call to action (subscribe, review, lead magnet, product).

## The method

1. **Titles:** 5 options. Specific benefit or tension, guest name if well known.
2. **Summary (100 to 150 words):** the problem discussed, why it matters, what the listener will learn. No spoilers of the best insight.
3. **Chapters:** timestamps every 3 to 10 minutes with clear labels (only from the transcript's real timestamps; if none, mark "approximate").
4. **Key takeaways:** 5 to 7 bullets, each one actionable.
5. **Best quotes:** 5 quotes, exact words, with timestamps and who said them.
6. **Guest bio:** 2 to 3 sentences, plus links mentioned.
7. **Resources mentioned:** books, tools, links.
8. **SEO description:** first 2 lines optimised for search, then the rest.
9. **Promotion pack:**
   - 3 short clip ideas with start and end timestamps and a hook caption for each.
   - 1 carousel outline from the takeaways.
   - 2 social posts (LinkedIn and Instagram or X).
   - Newsletter blurb (80 words).
10. **Call to action.**

## Rules

- Quotes must be exact. Never paraphrase inside quotation marks.
- Do not invent links or timestamps.
- Correct obvious transcription errors only when certain, otherwise flag them.
- Plain, inviting language.

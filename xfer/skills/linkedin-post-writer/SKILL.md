# LinkedIn Post Writer

On LinkedIn, the first 2 lines decide everything because the rest hides behind "see more". The best posts teach one thing through a specific experience, read easily on a phone, and end by inviting people in, not by begging for likes.

## What to collect

1. The idea, story, lesson, result or opinion.
2. Audience on LinkedIn (who should read and act).
3. The goal: authority, inbound leads, hiring, a launch, a resource download.
4. Proof or details: numbers, names (with permission), moments.
5. The user's voice (samples if available).

## Formats

- **Story:** moment, tension, turning point, lesson, question.
- **Lesson list:** "7 things I learned from 50 discovery calls", each line a real insight.
- **Contrarian take:** common belief, why it fails, what to do instead, nuance.
- **Framework:** named steps with one line each.
- **Case study:** client situation, what we did, result, takeaway.
- **Behind the scenes:** process, mistakes, numbers.
- **Carousel text:** slide by slide (cover, 6 to 8 content slides, CTA).

## Structure

1. **Hook (lines 1 and 2):** specific, surprising or tense. Under 200 characters total.
2. **Re-hook (line 3):** a short line that makes them keep reading.
3. **Body:** one idea, short lines, white space, concrete details.
4. **Takeaway:** one sentence they could quote.
5. **Ending:** a genuine question or a soft call to action (comment a keyword, DM, link in comments).

## Rules

- 120 to 250 words for most posts, up to 400 for stories.
- No hashtags overload: 0 to 3.
- No "I'm thrilled to announce", no humble brag without a lesson.
- Specific numbers and examples, true only.
- Write like you talk. No corporate jargon.
- Links in the first comment if reach matters.

## Output format

```
FORMAT: ...

POST (version A)
...

POST (version B, different hook and angle)
...

FIRST COMMENT
...

BEST TIME TO POST AND HOW TO ENGAGE
Reply to every comment in the first hour, ...
```

## Final check

- [ ] First 2 lines work alone
- [ ] One idea only
- [ ] Easy to read on a phone
- [ ] Ending invites conversation

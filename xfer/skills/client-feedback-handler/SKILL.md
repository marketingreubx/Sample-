# Client Feedback Handler

Feedback is rarely organised. It arrives in long messages, mixed with feelings, sometimes contradicting earlier approvals. This skill turns it into a clear checklist, protects the scope, and writes a reply that makes the client feel heard and the next steps obvious.

## What to collect

1. The feedback, exactly as received (paste everything, including voice note transcripts).
2. The deliverable it refers to and the original brief or approved direction.
3. The scope: revision rounds included and how many are used.
4. Deadline.

## The method

### 1. Read for emotion first
Is the client worried, frustrated, excited, rushed? The reply's first line should respond to that.

### 2. Sort every point
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Action | Clear change, in scope, will be done |
| Clarify | Unclear, needs a question |
| Discuss | Opinion or preference that might hurt the goal; needs a recommendation |
| Out of scope | New request beyond the agreement; needs a quote or later phase |
| Contradiction | Conflicts with earlier feedback or the brief |

### 3. Turn actions into a checklist
Specific, numbered, with where in the deliverable each change happens.

### 4. Write clarifying questions
Closed or multiple choice where possible: "Do you mean the font on the headline or on the whole page?"

### 5. Give a recommendation for opinions
Respect their taste, explain the impact on the goal, offer to try both if cheap.

### 6. Handle out of scope
Thank them for the idea, explain it is outside this round, give a price or suggest a later phase.

### 7. Write the reply
1. Acknowledge (one line).
2. What will change (the checklist).
3. Questions to answer.
4. Recommendations.
5. Out of scope items and options.
6. Revision count update and new delivery date.

## Rules

- Never argue about taste. Give reasons tied to goals.
- Always confirm in writing what will change.
- Track revision rounds clearly and kindly.
- If feedback contradicts an earlier approval, point to the approval politely.

## Output format

```
THE MOOD
...

SORTED FEEDBACK
| # | Point | Type | Notes |

YOUR CHECKLIST
1. ...

QUESTIONS FOR THE CLIENT
...

REPLY
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Every point sorted
- [ ] Contradictions flagged politely
- [ ] Out of scope handled with options
- [ ] Revision count and date confirmed

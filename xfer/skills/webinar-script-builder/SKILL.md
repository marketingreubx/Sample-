# Webinar Script Builder

A selling webinar teaches what and why, and sells the how. Each teaching point breaks a false belief that stops people buying. By the offer, the viewer already believes the result is possible, that this method is the way, and that they can do it with help.

## What to collect

1. Offer, price, guarantee, bonuses, deadline (real).
2. Audience and their biggest false beliefs (about the method, themselves, outside obstacles).
3. The user's story and proof.
4. Platform and length (45 to 90 minutes).
5. Goal: sales, calls booked, applications.

## The structure

1. **Title and promise:** "How to [result] without [pain] in [time]". 5 options.
2. **Opening (5 min):** welcome, promise, who it is for, what they will learn, a reason to stay to the end (bonus, Q&A).
3. **Origin story (5 min):** the user's struggle, discovery, result. Short and true.
4. **Secret 1, the vehicle (10 min):** why this method works and others fail.
5. **Secret 2, internal belief (10 min):** "I can do this" with a relatable example.
6. **Secret 3, external obstacle (10 min):** "time, money or tech won't stop me".
7. **Transition (2 min):** "Want help implementing this?"
8. **Offer (10 min):** what it is, each component and the outcome, bonuses, price anchored to value, payment options, guarantee, real deadline.
9. **Close (5 min):** two choices: keep doing it alone or get help; how to join.
10. **Q&A (10 to 20 min):** prepared answers to the top 8 objections, plus repeated CTA.

## Also include

- Slide outline (one idea per slide).
- Registration page copy (headline, 3 bullets, form CTA).
- Reminder emails: confirmation, 24 hours, 1 hour, live now.
- Replay and follow up: replay email, 2 to 3 offer emails until the deadline.
- Chat engagement prompts during the live.

## Rules

- Teach real value. People who do not buy should still win.
- Only real urgency and real results. Include income disclaimers where needed.
- Spoken language in the script.
- Keep the offer section under 25% of the time.

# Daily Time Blocker

A to-do list says what; a time-blocked day says when. Days fail when everything is treated as equally important and nothing has a time. This skill decides what matters, puts it in the right hours, and is honest about what will not fit.

## What to collect

1. Today's to-do list (everything on the mind).
2. Fixed commitments (calls, school runs, appointments) with times.
3. Working hours and when the user has the most energy.
4. Deadlines today or tomorrow.
5. The one result that would make today a success.

## The method

1. **Brain dump sort:** each task as Must (deadline or high impact), Should, Could, or Not today.
2. **Estimate** each task, then add 25%.
3. **Pick the one thing:** the task that moves the biggest goal.
4. **Build the day:**
   - Fixed commitments first.
   - Deep work block (60 to 120 minutes) in peak energy hours for the one thing.
   - Client deadlines next.
   - Batched shallow work: messages and email (2 to 3 short windows), admin, outreach block.
   - Breaks every 60 to 90 minutes, a real lunch.
   - 30 to 60 minutes of buffer.
   - Shutdown routine: review, plan tomorrow's top 3, close tabs.
5. **Fit check:** if planned time is more than available time, move Could and Should items, and say where they go.

## Rules

- Never fill more than 80% of the day.
- One deep work block minimum.
- Messages in windows, not all day.
- Be honest: show what moves to tomorrow.

## Output format

```
TODAY'S ONE THING
...

SORTED
Must: ... | Should: ... | Could: ... | Not today: ...

SCHEDULE
08:00 to 08:15 Plan and coffee
08:15 to 10:00 Deep work: ...
...

MOVED TO TOMORROW (or later)
...

SHUTDOWN ROUTINE
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] One thing in peak hours
- [ ] Breaks and buffer included
- [ ] Day is not overfilled
- [ ] Moved tasks listed

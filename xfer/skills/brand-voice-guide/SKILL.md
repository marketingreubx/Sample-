# Brand Voice Guide

A brand voice is a set of decisions about how the brand sounds, so that everyone writing for it (the owner, a VA, an agency, AI) produces content that feels like one person. Good guides are specific: they show real before and after examples, not just adjectives.

## What to collect

1. 3 to 10 samples of writing the user likes from their brand (posts, emails, website, DMs).
2. Who the audience is and how they talk.
3. Brand values and personality ("if the brand were a person...").
4. Brands they admire and ones they never want to sound like.
5. Words and phrases they love or hate.

## The method

### 1. Analyse the samples
Sentence length, formality, humour, use of "I" or "we", emoji, slang, punctuation habits, recurring phrases.

### 2. Personality (3 to 4 traits)
Each trait as "This, not that": "Confident, not arrogant", "Warm, not cheesy", "Clear, not technical".

### 3. Tone by situation
How the voice flexes: social posts, sales pages, DMs, customer support, apologies and bad news, celebrations.

### 4. Language rules
- Words and phrases to use (the brand's own vocabulary).
- Words to avoid (jargon, clichés, competitors' terms).
- Sentence style, reading level, punctuation, capitalisation.
- Emoji, slang, pidgin or local expressions: when and how much.
- Formatting: headings, bullets, length.

### 5. Examples
6 to 10 before-and-after rewrites across channels.

### 6. Cheat sheet
One page summary for a team or for pasting into AI tools.

### 7. Rewrite
If the user gives text, rewrite it in the voice and explain the changes.

## Rules

- Base the voice on real samples and the audience, not on generic adjectives.
- Every rule must be practical enough to apply.
- Keep it short enough that people actually use it.

## Output format

```
VOICE IN ONE LINE
...
PERSONALITY
1. ... not ...
TONE BY SITUATION
| Situation | Tone | Example |
USE / AVOID
...
STYLE RULES
...
BEFORE AND AFTER
...
CHEAT SHEET
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Based on samples
- [ ] "This, not that" traits
- [ ] Real before and after examples
- [ ] One page cheat sheet

# Retainer Offer Builder

Clients do not buy hours every month. They buy a result that keeps happening: steady bookings, consistent content, a site that never breaks, a pipeline that stays full. A good retainer names that ongoing result, makes delivery predictable, and makes cancelling feel like losing something.

## What to collect

1. The service and the ongoing result it creates.
2. Typical client and their monthly budget range.
3. What the user can deliver each month without burning out (hours, outputs).
4. Current one-off projects that could lead into a retainer.
5. Proof from past monthly work if any.

## The method

### 1. Name the monthly outcome
"Fully booked weekdays, managed for you" beats "12 posts per month". Write 3 options.

### 2. Define the deliverables
Outputs (posts, reports, updates, calls), service levels (response time, turnaround), and the strategy part (monthly planning, review). Make the strategy visible: it is what clients miss when they cancel.

### 3. Build 3 tiers
- **Essentials:** maintain.
- **Growth (recommended):** improve.
- **Partner:** lead, with priority and strategy.
Each with outcome, deliverables, communication, price. Price the middle at the user's target.

### 4. Terms
Minimum term (3 months is common for results-based work), billing date, payment upfront each month, notice period (30 days), what happens to unused deliverables (they do not roll over, or roll over once), price review every 6 or 12 months.

### 5. Rhythm
Onboarding in week 1, weekly or bi-weekly updates, monthly report and planning call, quarterly review with renewal conversation.

### 6. The pitch
- For existing project clients: after a win, "Want to keep this going every month?" with the outcome and the Growth tier.
- For new leads: present the retainer as the main offer and a one-off project as the alternative.
- Answers to: "Can we do month to month?", "Can we pause?", "Why a minimum term?".

## Rules

- The outcome must be something the user can influence every month.
- Do not promise unlimited anything.
- Include a clear way to leave. It increases trust and sign-ups.
- Use real proof only.

# Content Calendar Planner

A calendar is not a list of random ideas. It is a plan that moves followers toward the user's offer: attract, build trust, convert, repeat. It must also be realistic, or it gets abandoned by week two.

## What to collect

1. Business, offer, price, and the goal for the month (bookings, leads, launch, followers, authority).
2. Audience: who, their problems, desires, objections.
3. Platforms and formats the user can actually make (Reels, carousels, Stories, talking head, faceless).
4. How many hours a week for content.
5. Key dates: launches, promotions, holidays, events.
6. What has worked before (top posts).

## The method

### 1. Pillars (3 to 5)
Example: Educate (tips), Prove (results and behind the scenes), Connect (story and values), Sell (offer and FAQs), Engage (questions, trends). Assign a percentage that matches the goal (more Sell in a launch month).

### 2. Rhythm
Posts per week per platform the user can sustain, plus daily Stories. Better 3 good posts a week than 7 rushed ones.

### 3. Weekly themes
Each week focuses on one problem or topic the offer solves, building toward the offer moment.

### 4. The calendar (day by day)
For each post: date, platform, format, pillar, topic, hook, key point, call to action (save, comment keyword, DM, link), and a matching Story idea.

### 5. Conversion moments
At least one post per week with a direct call to action (DM keyword, booking link, lead magnet).

### 6. Batching plan
One batching session per week: film all Reels, design carousels, write captions, schedule. Include a shot list.

### 7. Measure
Which metrics matter for this goal (saves and shares for reach, DMs and link clicks for sales) and a weekly 15 minute review.

## Rules

- Every post connects to a pillar and the monthly goal.
- Mix formats, but only ones the user can make.
- Specific topics, not "motivation Monday".
- Leave 2 flexible slots per month for trends and replies to comments.

# Project Planner

Plans fail when they are vague, optimistic or forgotten. This skill makes the outcome concrete, breaks work into tasks small enough to start today, adds realistic time and buffer, and sets up a weekly rhythm so the plan is used.

## What to collect

1. The goal and why it matters.
2. Deadline or desired timeline.
3. People involved and their availability.
4. Budget and tools.
5. Known constraints, dependencies and risks.

## The method

### 1. Define done
Outcome in one sentence, measurable definition of done, what is out of scope.

### 2. Phases and milestones
3 to 6 phases, each ending in a visible milestone.

### 3. Task breakdown
Tasks under each phase, each:
- Starts with a verb.
- Takes half a day to 3 days (split bigger ones).
- Has an owner, estimate, and dependencies.

### 4. Timeline
Sequence tasks, account for dependencies and availability, add 20% to 30% buffer, highlight the critical path.

### 5. Risks
Top 5 risks, likelihood, impact, mitigation, early warning sign.

### 6. Operating rhythm
Weekly 30 minute review (done, blocked, next), daily top 3 for solo users, milestone check-ins.

### 7. Tracker
Columns: task, phase, owner, start, due, status, notes. Suggested tool (Notion, Trello, Google Sheets).

### 8. This week
The first 5 tasks to start now.

## Rules

- Realistic estimates; most people underestimate by 30% to 50%.
- No task without an owner.
- Keep the plan on one page where possible.

## Output format

```
OUTCOME
... Done means: ... Not included: ...

PHASES AND MILESTONES
...

TASKS
| Task | Phase | Owner | Estimate | Depends on | Due |

TIMELINE
Week 1: ... (critical path marked)

RISKS
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Warning sign |

WEEKLY RHYTHM
...

START THIS WEEK
1. ...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Clear definition of done
- [ ] Tasks small, owned and estimated
- [ ] Buffer included
- [ ] First actions for this week

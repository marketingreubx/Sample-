# Objection Handler

An objection is usually a question in disguise: "Is this worth it?", "Will it work for me?", "Can I trust you?", "Is now the right time?". The skill is to find the real question, answer it honestly, and offer an easy next step. Arguing loses. Curiosity wins.

## What to collect

1. The exact words the prospect used (paste the message) and the channel.
2. The offer, price and what is included.
3. Where the conversation was before the objection.
4. Anything known about their situation, budget or timeline.

## The response pattern: A.C.A.R.

1. **Acknowledge:** show you heard them, without agreeing that it is a problem. "Totally fair to ask."
2. **Clarify:** ask one question to find the real concern. "When you say too much, is it the total, or doing it all at once?"
3. **Answer:** address the real concern with value, proof, a different option, or honesty.
4. **Re-close:** offer one easy next step. "Would splitting it into two payments help?"

In DMs, send Acknowledge + Clarify first and wait for the answer before replying further.

## The objection library

**"It's too expensive."** Real concerns: value not clear, cash flow, comparing to cheaper options. Answers: reconnect to the cost of the problem, payment plan, smaller starting package (less scope, not a discount), proof from a similar client.

**"I need to think about it."** Real concerns: unanswered question, fear, low urgency. Ask what specifically, answer it, agree a decision date.

**"Not right now."** Ask what would need to change, note the time they give, schedule the follow up, send something useful meanwhile.

**"I need to ask my partner or boss."** Offer a one page summary or to join the conversation, ask what they think the partner will ask.

**"I already have someone."** Respect it, ask what is working and what they wish was better, offer to be a backup or second opinion.

**"I can do it myself."** Agree it is possible, compare time and results honestly, offer a DIY tip, leave the door open.

**"Send me more info."** Ask what they want to know so you send the right thing, then send something short and specific with a next step.

**"Does it really work?"** Show relevant proof, explain the process, offer a guarantee or a small first step.

**"Can you lower the price?"** Hold the price, offer a scope trade or payment option.

**Ghosting after the price.** Day 1: light check-in. Day 3: helpful extra. Day 7: easy yes-or-no close. Day 30: fresh start.

## Rules

- Never pressure, guilt or invent scarcity.
- Never drop the price without removing scope.
- Never criticise competitors.
- If the offer genuinely is not a fit, say so. It builds reputation.
- Keep DM replies short.

## Output format

```
WHAT THEY REALLY MEAN
...

REPLY (send now)
...

IF THEY ANSWER ...
- "...": ...

IF THEY GO QUIET
Day 1: ... Day 3: ... Day 7: ...

ON A CALL, SAY
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Real concern identified
- [ ] A.C.A.R. followed
- [ ] One easy next step
- [ ] Honest, no pressure

# Testimonial Collector

"Great to work with!" does not sell. A strong testimonial names the situation before, the doubt they had, what changed, and who they would recommend it to. Clients rarely write that unprompted, so the questions do the work.

## What to collect

1. The client, project and results.
2. Where the testimonial will be used (website, sales page, Instagram, Google, proposals).
3. Format wanted: written, video, voice note, screenshot.
4. Raw feedback if the user already has some (paste it).

## The method

### 1. Timing
Ask right after a visible win or at the end of a successful project, when enthusiasm is highest. Never with an unpaid invoice or an open problem.

### 2. The request message
Short, personal, mentions the result, explains it takes 3 minutes, gives the options (reply here, voice note, form, Google link), and promises to send the edited version for approval.

### 3. The 5 questions
1. What was going on before we worked together?
2. What made you hesitate before hiring me (if anything)?
3. What changed? Any numbers or moments that stood out?
4. What was it like working together?
5. Who would you recommend this to?

### 4. Video testimonial script (60 to 90 seconds)
Prompts in the same order, plus filming tips: phone vertical, face a window, quiet room, 2 takes.

### 5. Turn replies into proof
- **Headline quote** (1 line).
- **Short quote** (2 to 3 sentences) for bios, ads and DMs.
- **Long quote** (5 to 7 sentences) for sales pages.
- **Mini case study** (before, after, numbers).
- **Objection tag:** which buyer doubt each quote answers (price, trust, results, time).
Light edits only: grammar and length. Never change meaning or add claims.

### 6. Permission
Ask for approval of the edited version, and permission to use their name, photo, business name and link.

### 7. Follow ups
Day 3 gentle reminder, day 7 offer to draft it for them to approve.

## Rules

- Never write fake testimonials or put words in a client's mouth.
- Keep the client's voice. Fix only clarity and length.
- Follow review platform rules (no incentives for Google reviews in many cases).

## Output format

```
REQUEST MESSAGE
...

THE 5 QUESTIONS (form or message)
...

VIDEO PROMPTS AND TIPS
...

FOLLOW UPS
...

FROM THEIR REPLY
Headline: "..."
Short: "..."
Long: "..."
Mini case study: ...
Answers the objection: ...

APPROVAL AND PERMISSION MESSAGE
...
```

## Final check

- [ ] Questions draw out before and after
- [ ] Edits keep the client's meaning
- [ ] Permission requested
- [ ] Quotes matched to objections

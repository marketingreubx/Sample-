# LinkedIn Profile Optimizer

Most freelancer profiles read like a CV, so they attract recruiters. A client-attracting profile reads like a landing page: it tells the right buyer, in seconds, who it is for, what changes for them, why to trust this person and what to do next.

## What to collect

1. Current headline, About, experience and featured items (pasted text or screenshots).
2. Who the user wants to attract and the result they deliver.
3. Proof: client results, testimonials, case studies, recognisable names, certifications.
4. The next step they want visitors to take (book a call, DM, download a resource, visit site).
5. Tone: formal, warm, bold.

## The method

### 1. Headline (220 characters max)
Formula: who you help + result + how or proof. Give 5 options.
"I help B2B SaaS founders turn LinkedIn into a pipeline | Ghostwriting + content systems | 40+ founders served"
Avoid "Seasoned professional", "Passionate about", job titles alone.

### 2. Banner text
One line promise plus a call to action, readable on mobile, kept clear of the profile photo area on the left.

### 3. About section (1,500 to 2,200 characters)
1. Hook in the first 2 lines (shown before "see more"): name the reader's problem or desired result.
2. Who you help and what you do, in plain words.
3. How you do it: 3 to 5 bullets of the method or services.
4. Proof: 2 to 3 short results or a mini story.
5. Who it is not for (builds trust).
6. Clear call to action with how to reach you.
Write in first person, short paragraphs, no keyword stuffing.

### 4. Featured section
Recommend 3 items in order: a case study or result post, a lead magnet or booking link, and a best performing post. Write the title and description for each.

### 5. Experience entries
Rewrite the current freelance or business role as a client-facing description: who you serve, what you deliver, results. Convert past jobs into relevant credibility in 2 to 3 bullets each.

### 6. Skills and settings
Top 3 pinned skills that match buyer searches. Turn on Creator mode or Services if relevant. Custom URL suggestion.

### 7. 30 day activity plan
3 posts a week (topics listed), 10 thoughtful comments a day on buyers' posts, 5 connection requests a day with a short personal note.

## Rules

- Write for buyers, not recruiters. Outcomes over duties.
- Only real results and real client names the user can share.
- Keep each paragraph to 1 to 3 lines.
- No emojis overload. One or two as bullets at most, only if the user's tone allows.

## Final check

- [ ] Headline names the who and the result
- [ ] First 2 lines of About hook the buyer
- [ ] Clear call to action
- [ ] Only verifiable proof

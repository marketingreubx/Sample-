# Social Media Audit

An audit is only useful if it is specific, honest and ranked. "Post more consistently" is not an insight. "Your last 9 posts have no call to action and your bio link goes to an old page" is. This skill scores the profile, shows evidence for every point, and ranks fixes by impact.

## What to collect

1. Profile screenshots (bio, highlights, grid, a few posts and insights) or pasted data.
2. The account's goal: bookings, sales, leads, brand awareness, community.
3. Audience and offer.
4. Insights if available: reach, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks, follower growth.

Only judge what is visible or provided. Say what is missing.

## The scorecard (100 points)

| Area | Points | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Bio and name | 15 | Who you help, result, proof, CTA, searchable name field |
| Profile photo and highlights | 10 | Clear face or logo, highlights for services, proof, FAQ, booking |
| Grid and branding | 10 | Recognisable style, readable covers |
| Content mix | 15 | Pillars covering educate, prove, connect, sell |
| Hooks and first frames | 15 | Specific, stop the scroll |
| Captions | 10 | Value, engagement trigger, CTA |
| Consistency | 10 | Steady rhythm, recent posts |
| Engagement quality | 5 | Saves, shares, real comments, replies from the account |
| Conversion path | 10 | Clear way to DM, book or buy, working link |

## The method

1. Score each area with evidence (quote or describe what was seen).
2. List strengths (keep doing these).
3. List problems ranked by impact on the goal.
4. Rewrite the bio (3 options) and name field for search.
5. Suggest highlights structure and covers.
6. Give 5 post ideas based on what already works.
7. 30 day action plan: week 1 quick fixes, weeks 2 to 4 content changes, metrics to watch.

## Rules

- Evidence for every point.
- Kind but honest. No vague advice.
- Do not assume metrics that were not shared.
- Benchmark engagement carefully; it varies by niche and size.

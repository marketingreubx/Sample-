# Ad Copy Writer

Ads interrupt people who were not looking for you. Good ad copy earns attention with a sharp hook, makes one promise to one audience, proves it, and gives one easy action. The best performers come from testing different angles, not polishing one ad.

## What to collect

1. Offer, price, landing page or destination (form, WhatsApp, DM, booking).
2. Audience and their awareness level (cold, warm, retargeting).
3. Platform and format (image, carousel, video, search).
4. Proof: results, testimonials, numbers, guarantees.
5. Budget and goal (leads, bookings, sales, messages).
6. Platform restrictions in the niche (health, finance, beauty claims).

## The method

### 1. Five angles
- **Pain:** the problem and its cost.
- **Desire:** the outcome they want.
- **Proof:** a result or testimonial up front.
- **Objection killer:** answers the main doubt (price, time, trust).
- **Offer:** the deal, bonus or free step.

### 2. For each angle (Meta and TikTok)
- Hook (first line or first 2 seconds).
- Primary text: short version (under 125 characters visible) and long version (story, proof, offer, CTA).
- Headline (under 40 characters).
- Description.
- Call to action button.
- Creative direction: what the image or video shows, text overlay.

### 3. Video ad script (15 to 30 seconds)
Hook (0 to 3s), problem (3 to 8s), solution and proof (8 to 20s), offer and CTA (20 to 30s), with captions and shots.

### 4. Google Search
3 to 5 ad groups by intent, 10 to 15 headlines (30 characters), 4 descriptions (90 characters), keywords and negatives.

### 5. Test plan
Test angles first (one ad set, 3 to 5 ads), then hooks, then creatives. Budget per test, how long to run (until each ad has enough results to judge, commonly 3 to 7 days), and what to measure (cost per result, click-through rate, conversion rate). Kill rules and scale rules.

## Rules

- One audience, one promise, one action per ad.
- Follow platform advertising policies: no personal attribute callouts ("Are you overweight?"), no before-and-after claims where banned, no guaranteed income or health outcomes.
- True claims only, with proof the user provided.
- Mobile first, readable in 3 seconds.

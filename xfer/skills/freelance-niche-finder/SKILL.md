# Freelance Niche Finder

A niche is a group of people with the same expensive problem who can be found easily and who can pay. Picking one makes content, outreach and pricing easier. This skill avoids guesswork by scoring options and then testing the best one with real conversations within a week.

## What to collect

1. The user's skills and services.
2. Industries they have worked in, past clients, hobbies, communities they belong to.
3. Location and languages, and whether they want local or global clients.
4. Income goal per month and how many clients they can serve.

## The method

### 1. Generate 10 candidate niches
Combine a skill with a specific audience: "content for physiotherapy clinics", "Shopify email for skincare brands", "LinkedIn ghostwriting for B2B founders in fintech". Pull from experience first (unfair advantage), then from markets with obvious money.

### 2. Score each from 1 to 5
| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Pain | Is the problem urgent and costly for them? |
| Budget | Do they already spend money on solving it? |
| Reach | Can you find 100 of them this week (hashtags, directories, groups, Google Maps)? |
| Fit | Do you understand them, and do you enjoy them? |
| Proof | Do you have, or can you quickly get, a relevant result? |
| Competition | Is it served badly enough for you to stand out? (5 = poorly served) |
| Math | Can 3 to 6 clients at their budget hit the income goal? |

### 3. Pick the top 2 and sanity check
For each: average budget, the price the user would charge, number of clients needed for the goal, and where exactly they hang out.

### 4. Validate in 7 days
- Day 1: list 30 prospects in each niche.
- Days 2 to 5: send 10 messages a day using a research question, not a pitch: "I'm researching how [niche] get [result]. What's the hardest part for you right now?"
- Track replies and the words they use.
- Day 7 decision rule: choose the niche with more replies and more pain language. If neither gets 10% replies, adjust the audience or the problem and test again.

### 5. Position
Write the niche statement, bio line and offer idea for the winner.

## Rules

- A niche can be an audience, a problem or a platform, but it must be specific.
- Do not pick a niche only because it pays well if the user has no access or interest.
- Remind the user a niche is a starting point. They can widen later once they have proof.
- Never claim market sizes without a source.

## Final check

- [ ] Every candidate is specific (skill plus audience or problem)
- [ ] Scores explained, not random
- [ ] Validation uses real conversations, not opinions
- [ ] Clear go or no go rule

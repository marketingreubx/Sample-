# Outreach Sequence Planner

Clients come from volume times quality times follow up. This skill works backwards from the user's income goal to a daily number, then designs a sequence where every touch has a clear purpose, and ends with the numbers to review each week.

## What to collect

1. Offer and price, and how many new clients the user needs per month.
2. Target niche and where they can be found.
3. Channels available (Instagram, WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn, phone, in person).
4. Time per day for outreach.
5. Current reply and close rates if known.

## The method

### 1. Do the math
Use the user's numbers or these starting assumptions (label them):
- Reply rate: 10% to 20% for personalised DMs, 3% to 8% for cold email.
- Replies to call: 25% to 40%.
- Call to client: 20% to 30%.
Clients needed ÷ close rate ÷ call rate ÷ reply rate = contacts needed per month. Divide by working days for the daily target. Show the calculation.

### 2. Build the list
Where to find prospects (hashtags, location tags, Google Maps, directories, groups, marketplace reviews, competitors' followers), the qualifying criteria, and the daily research routine (how many to add per day).

### 3. Design the sequence (typical 21 days)
| Day | Channel | Touch | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Instagram | Like 2 posts, thoughtful comment | Familiarity |
| 1 | DM | Personal opener with observation | Start conversation |
| 3 | DM | Light follow up | Resurface |
| 5 | Email (if found) | Short value email | Second channel |
| 8 | DM | Free tip or mini audit offer | Give value |
| 14 | Comment + DM | Reference a new post | Stay relevant |
| 21 | DM or email | Polite close | Final reply trigger |
Adjust to the user's channels. Explain the purpose of each step.

### 4. Write the core messages
Provide the opener, each follow up and the close as short templates with [brackets] for personal details.

### 5. Tracker
Columns: name, handle, link, niche, channel, observation, day 0 date, last touch, next touch, stage, notes. Stages: To contact, Sent, Replied, Call booked, Proposal, Client, Not now, No.

### 6. Weekly review
Contacts made, reply rate, calls booked, closes, best performing opener, what to change next week.

## Rules

- Personalisation beats volume, but both are needed. Never plan more daily messages than the user can personalise.
- Respect platform limits (new Instagram accounts: 20 to 30 new DMs a day; warm up gradually).
- Stop the sequence the moment someone replies, and switch to conversation.
- Never plan spam, scraping of private data or fake accounts.

- [ ] Every touch has a purpose
- [ ] Within platform limits

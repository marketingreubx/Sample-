# Discovery Call Planner

The person asking questions leads the call. A good discovery call feels like a doctor's appointment: diagnose first, prescribe second. The prospect should talk 70% of the time, and by the end both sides should know whether it is a fit and what happens next.

## What to collect

1. The offer, packages and prices.
2. What is known about the prospect (form answers, DMs, website, profile).
3. Call length and format (video, phone, WhatsApp).
4. The goal of this call: close on the call, send a proposal, book a second call.

## Before the call

- Research checklist (5 minutes): their business, recent posts, website, visible problems, competitors.
- Confirmation message 24 hours before, with the agenda and a question to think about.
- Prepare: 2 relevant case studies, the price options, a payment link.

## The call flow (30 to 45 minutes)

1. **Open (2 min):** thanks, agenda, permission. "By the end, we'll know if it makes sense to work together. Sound good?"
2. **Situation (5 to 8 min):** where are you now, what does a normal week look like, what is working.
3. **Goals (5 min):** what do you want in 3 to 6 months, why does that matter, what would it change for you personally.
4. **Pain and obstacles (5 to 8 min):** what is in the way, what have you tried, why didn't it work.
5. **Cost of inaction (3 min):** what happens if nothing changes in 6 months, what is it costing now.
6. **Budget, timeline, decision (3 min):** "Have you set aside a budget for this?", "When do you want this sorted?", "Who else is involved in the decision?"
7. **Summary (2 min):** reflect back in their words, confirm it is right.
8. **Prescribe (5 min):** "Based on what you shared, here's what I'd recommend..." The plan in 3 steps, tied to their goals.
9. **Price (2 min):** state it once with options, then stop talking.
10. **Objections (as needed):** acknowledge, clarify, answer, re-close.
11. **Close (2 min):** "Want to go ahead? I can send the payment link now and we start on [date]." Or a clear next step with a date.

## Question bank

Write 15 to 20 questions grouped by stage, tailored to the user's niche.

## After the call

- Same day recap email: their goals in their words, the plan, the investment, next step, the link.
- Follow up plan if no decision: day 2, day 5, day 10.
- Notes template: goals, pains, budget, timeline, decision makers, objections, next step.

## Rules

- Do not pitch before the summary.
- One question at a time. Let silence work.
- If it is not a fit, say so and refer them elsewhere.
- Never invent results or pressure them to decide on the call.

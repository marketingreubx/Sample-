# Email Sequence Writer

A sequence is a planned conversation. Each email has one job (build trust, teach one thing, handle one objection, make one offer) and leads naturally to the next. Short, personal, one call to action.

## What to collect

1. Sequence type and goal (book calls, sell a product, get replies, onboard buyers).
2. The offer, price and link, plus any deadline that is real.
3. The audience and how they joined the list (which lead magnet or page).
4. The sender's voice and a few stories, results or lessons to draw from.
5. Email tool limits if any (plain text versus designed).

## Sequence blueprints

**Welcome (5 emails, days 0, 1, 3, 5, 7):** deliver the freebie and set expectations; the sender's story and why they care; one big quick win; a common mistake and the better way; a soft invitation to the offer.

**Lead magnet follow up (5 emails over 7 days):** delivery and first step; did you try it plus a tip; case study; objection handling; invitation.

**Launch (7 to 10 emails):** pre-launch story and problem; open cart with the offer; proof and case study; FAQ and objections; bonus or deadline reason; last day (2 or 3 emails: morning, afternoon, final hours).

**Sales follow up after a call:** recap and proposal; proof; answer the likely objection; last check-in.

**Abandoned checkout (3 emails):** reminder plus help; answer the top question; final note.

**Post purchase (4 emails):** welcome and first step; quick win; check-in and support; review or referral request.

**Re-engagement (3 emails):** "Still want these?"; best resource; unsubscribe or stay.

## Writing each email

- **Subject line:** 2 to 7 words, curiosity or clear benefit, lowercase friendly. Give 2 options.
- **Preview text:** completes or teases the subject.
- **Opening line:** start in the middle of a story or with a direct statement. No "I hope you're well".
- **Body:** 80 to 250 words, one idea, short paragraphs, conversational.
- **One call to action:** a link or "reply with YES". Repeat the link once at most.
- **P.S.:** optional, for the deadline or a second hook.

## Rules

- Every email must be useful even if they never buy.
- Real deadlines and real scarcity only.
- No invented testimonials, incomes or results.
- Include the unsubscribe reminder only where the tool does not add it automatically.
- Write like one person to one person.

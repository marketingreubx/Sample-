# Caption Formula

Match the caption length of the original's format: short for ASMR/POV/skits (1–3 lines), medium for education/story (5–10 lines).

## Structure
1. **Line 1 — hook** (shows before "…more"): different wording from the on-screen hook, same idea. Under 125 characters.
2. **Value or story** (1–4 short lines): the why, a mini story, or the tip in writing. Line breaks, no walls of text.
3. **Search keywords**, worked in naturally: service + location + problem (e.g. "dermaplaning in Abuja", "acne facial Gwarinpa"). Instagram search reads captions more than hashtags.
4. **One CTA**: the comment-keyword mechanic if the original used one or the goal is leads ("Comment GLOW and I'll send you prices"); otherwise save/share ("Send this to the friend who…").
5. **3–5 hashtags max**: 1 niche, 1–2 location, 1 service, 1 brand.

## Platform tweaks (same structure, different packaging)
| | Instagram | TikTok | YouTube Shorts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Length | 1–10 lines by format | 1–3 lines; the video carries it | Title ≤ 60 chars + 1–2 line description |
| Keywords | In the caption sentences | In the caption **and** the on-screen text (TikTok search reads both) | In the title |
| Hashtags | 3–5 | 3–5 (niche + location + service; skip #fyp) | 1–3 |
| CTA | Comment keyword or share | Comment keyword, "save this", or reply-with-video bait | "Subscribe for…" or comment keyword |

## Rules
- One CTA only. No "call, DM, visit our website and click the link" stacks.
- No phone number dumps unless the user asks.
- Emojis: 0–3, purposeful.
- Write like a person, not a brand. First person if a person is on camera.
- Never copy the original's caption sentences; keep only its mechanic.

## DM reply for the comment keyword
After the caption, write the DM to send people who comment the keyword: 1–2 sentences that deliver the promised thing and ask one question to start a conversation (e.g. "Here's our facial menu 💆🏾‍♀️ [link]. What's your main skin concern right now: acne, dark spots or dullness?").

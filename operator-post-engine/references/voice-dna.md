# Voice DNA

Study notes from the top posts of @AlexHormozi, @LeilaHormozi and @naval (scraped October 2026, ranked by likes and bookmarks). Quotes are for **studying the structure only**; never reuse them in output.

## What the data says (all three)

- **Short wins likes; lists and frameworks win bookmarks.** One-liners get the most likes and reposts. Lists, routines and frameworks get the most bookmarks (Leila's "5 reasons" list: 6.1k bookmarks; her "daily routine" opener: 5.7k bookmarks).
- **The top posts are reframes.** Each takes something the reader already believes and flips or sharpens it.
- **No hashtags, no links in the best text posts, almost no emojis.**
- **Personal life posts break the pattern.** Leila's pregnancy and life posts ("9 date nights left until it's 3 of us") pulled 600k–800k views with 3–8 words. Being human is a format too.

## Naval

**Topics:** wealth, leverage, technology, AI, markets vs mobs, freedom, society.
**Tone:** detached, aphoristic, compressed, symmetrical. Observes instead of instructing. No exclamation marks.

| Pattern | Real example (study only) | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Evolution triad | "A 'computer' used to be a job title. / Then a computer became a thing humans used. / Now a computer is becoming a thing computers use." | 39M views |
| Category metaphor | "Capitalism is PvE. The alternatives all degenerate to PvP." | 16.9M views |
| Insurance chain | "Bitcoin is insurance against fiat. / ZCash is insurance against Bitcoin." | 4.4M views |
| Race / tension framing | "It's a race between technology-driven abundance and government-driven poverty." | 16k |
| Correction | "Crowds aren't wise - markets are wise, and mobs are dumb." | 10.5k |
| Three-attribute analysis | "Drones are easy to make, hard to stop, and bring Mutually Assured Destruction to conventional warfare." | 1.9M views |
| Wry one-liner | "The latest IQ test involves data centers and water." | 1.9M views |

**Fingerprints:** symmetry and chiasmus, "X is Y", parallel clauses, borrowed vocab (games, insurance, races), no "you should", leaves the reader to finish the thought.

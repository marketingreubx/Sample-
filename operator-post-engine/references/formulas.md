# Post Formulas

Each formula: skeleton → why it works → niche examples. Example niches: **SMM** = social media managers; **ESTH** = estheticians and spa owners growing their business.

## A. One-liners (likes and reposts)

**1. The Underrated Skill**
`The most underrated skill in [niche] is the ability to [unsexy behaviour] when [hard condition].`
Why: turns a boring habit into a status skill.
- SMM: The most underrated skill in social media management is the ability to post the 40th video with the same energy as the first.
- ESTH: The most underrated skill in this industry isn't extractions. It's rebooking the client before she leaves the bed.

**2. Not X. It's Y.**
`[Outcome] isn't [what they blame]. It's [real cause].`
- SMM: Your page isn't shadowbanned. It's boring.
- ESTH: Empty appointment books aren't a marketing problem. They're a follow-up problem.

**3. The Contrast Couplet**
`[Weak group] [does A]. / [Strong group] [does B].`
- SMM: Amateurs post to be seen. Pros post to be chosen.
- ESTH: Average estheticians sell facials. Booked-out estheticians sell results.

**4. Paradox Advice**
`The fastest way to [goal] is to [opposite-sounding action].`
- SMM: The fastest way to get more clients is to serve fewer niches.
- ESTH: The fastest way to raise your prices is to stop offering 14 services.

**5. Redefinition**
`Most people think [word] means [X]. It doesn't. [Word] is [Y].`
- SMM: Most people think consistency means posting daily. It doesn't. Consistency is posting the same message until your market repeats it back to you.

**6. The Math Reframe**
`[Outcome] isn't luck. It's math. [Simple equation].`
- SMM: $3K a month isn't luck. It's math. 3 clients × $1,000. Most people need 3 yeses, not 3,000 followers.
- ESTH: A full calendar isn't luck. It's math. 20 regulars × 1 visit a month = you never chase clients again.

**7. Hidden Cost / Unseen Work**
`Nobody sees [hidden work]. They only see [visible result].`
- ESTH: Nobody sees the 200 faces you practised on. They just see your "natural talent".

**8. Labelled Hack + Chiasmus**
`10/10 [niche] hack: [verb A] [thing] before [thing] [verb A]s you.`
- SMM: 10/10 freelancer hack: Fire bad clients before bad clients fire your peace.

**9. Wry Observation (Naval)**
`[Ironic fact about the industry, said deadpan].`
- SMM: The fastest-growing skill in marketing is making a phone look like a camera crew.

**10. Category Metaphor (Naval)**
`[Thing A] is [game/insurance/race term]. [Thing B] is [contrasting term].`
- SMM: Ads are rent. Content is equity.
- ESTH: Discounts are a loan against your reputation.

## B. Short multi-line (likes plus some bookmarks)

**11. Evolution Triad (Naval)**
`[X] used to be [A]. / Then it became [B]. / Now it's [C].`
- SMM: Social media used to be where you posted your business. / Then it became how you marketed your business. / Now it is your business.

**12. Two-Beat Slap (Alex)**
`[Hard truth with three adjectives]. / [2–3 word command].`
- SMM: Your first 30 posts will flop, embarrass you and teach you everything. / Post them anyway.

**13. Claim → Observation → Rule (Leila)**
```
[Bold claim].
I've seen [specific observation from experience].
[Rule in 5–7 words].
```
- SMM: Overplanning has killed more content careers than bad content. / I've watched talented creators spend 3 weeks on a logo and 0 days posting. / Done and posted beats perfect and pending.

**14. Diagnosis (Alex)**
`If you're [doing visible activity] and you're not [getting result], you have [real problem]. [1–2 lines explaining].`
- ESTH: If you're posting every day and still not booked, the problem isn't visibility. It's that nobody can tell what you're best at.

**15. Emotional Crescendo**
`[Hard]. [Harder]. [Hardest]. That's when [turn]. And that's when [payoff].`

**16. Stacked Parallel Lines**
```
[Adjective] with [noun].
[Adjective] with [noun].
… (4–5 lines)
```
- SMM: Picky with clients. / Clear with scope. / Fast with replies. / Slow with discounts. / Obsessed with results.

## C. Lists (bookmarks)

**17. Ranked List, "in order"**
`Most common reasons [audience] fail at [goal], in order:` then 5–6 numbered items, each ≤ 6 words, escalating.
- SMM: Most common reasons new SMMs stay broke, in order: 1) No niche 2) No offer 3) No outreach 4) Underpricing 5) Quitting at month 3 6) Thinking it's the algorithm

**18. "N reasons you should [scary action]" with savage bullets**
5 bullets mixing one brutal line, one practical line and one identity line.
- ESTH: 5 reasons to raise your prices this month: / - Tired hands give tired facials. / - Cheap clients complain the most. / - Your skill went up. Your price didn't. / - Busy and broke is not a business. / - The right clients are waiting for you to look premium.

**19. Do / Don't Pairs**
`Don't [common move]. [Better move].` × 4–5.

## D. Story and authority (follows and threads)

**20. Failure Résumé** *(requires the user's real numbers)*
`I've [failed X times at A]. / I've [been rejected Y times]. / My first [Z] [things] got [bad result]. / [Turn]. / [Lesson].`

**21. Identity + Proof + Promise (thread opener)**
`I'm [age/role]. / I [credible result]. / Here's [routine/framework/how]:`

**22. Vulnerable Origin (thread opener)**
`I used to be awful at [skill]. / Then I found [one framework]. / It [result]. Here it is:`

**23. Thread Title + Objection Killer (Naval)**
`How to [desired result] (without [biggest objection]):`
- SMM: How to get your first 3 retainer clients (without a big following):
- ESTH: How to fill your calendar in 30 days (without running discounts):

**24. Personal Moment** *(human posts, once a week)*
3–10 words about a real milestone, with 0–2 emojis. Let the image or moment carry it.

## Thread skeleton (from any formula)
1. Hook (formula 21, 22 or 23)
2. The core belief to break
3–6. One step or lesson per tweet: a bold first line, then 1–2 lines of proof or example
7. Recap in 3 bullets
8. Soft CTA: "If this helped, follow @handle. I write about [topic] for [audience]."
